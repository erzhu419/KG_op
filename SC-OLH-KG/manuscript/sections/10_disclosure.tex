\section{Code and Data Disclosure}\label{sec:code-data}

Code and the declared seeds generate every synthetic instance.  The Energy
study uses the public data cited in Section~\ref{sec:experiments}.  An
anonymous code-and-data attachment, \nolinkurl{or_submission_revision_controls_20261002.zip},
contains source code, original and supplementary protocols, compact
per-task results, the processed Energy archive, and commands to regenerate
analyses, tables, figures, and PDFs.  It also contains the Lean source and
its pinned dependency configuration.  Original protocol metadata errors are
documented in a separate erratum.  Historical observations are preserved;
corrected Energy V5, mechanism attribution and new-family confirmation have
separate protocols and per-task artifacts.
The attachment also includes the postdecision remainder and first-center
diagnostics, and compact public-battery replay outcomes and census summaries.
Battery window-level replay requires the retained population journals, which
are outside this compact attachment.

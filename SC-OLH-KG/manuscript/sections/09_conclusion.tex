\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We developed a source-scored structural initial design for chance-constrained
ordered policy profiles.  Replicated source outcomes rank a public library;
farthest-first coverage selects ten profiles in a cross-grid coordinate; a
replaceable backend searches; and an independent verifier certifies or
abstains.  Finite guarantees cover discretization, selection, rank recovery,
conditional transfer, and verification.

On the registered task law, source scoring improves coverage and certification
over generic structure, but reverses in adverse regimes and held-out
Energy and loses at equal one-target cost.  It is therefore an amortized,
task-law-dependent investment, not a universal remedy.

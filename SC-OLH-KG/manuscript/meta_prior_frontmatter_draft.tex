\TITLE{Source-Learned Structural Priors for Chance-Constrained Simulation Optimization}

\RUNTITLE{Source-Learned Structural Priors for Chance Constraints}

\ABSTRACT{We study how replicated simulations from related tasks can inform a
new chance-constrained simulation optimization problem when policies are
represented by ordered profiles.  A finite public function dictionary is used
to learn response and cumulative-risk structure across source tasks.  A target
task then updates the source structure and function posteriors using paid
observations, while a correlated mean--risk knowledge-gradient rule allocates
the remaining evaluation and verification budget.  The source archive contains
8 tasks, 64 policies, and 12 repetitions per policy; the target protocols use
the same 8-point initial design and fixed paired budgets.  Across the frozen
mechanism and total-budget studies, the structural prior improves early target
prediction and decision utility under matched mechanisms, while target evidence
can downweight a mismatched source structure.  The gain is not universal:
generic target learning catches up at larger budgets, risk-structure gains are
small and unstable, and the current operational and mature-optimizer loops do
not establish an independent deployment advantage.  These results support a
bounded claim for source-learned structural meta-priors within a declared
representation and dictionary, and identify risk calibration, candidate
coverage, task feasibility, and knowledge-gradient cost as the remaining
limits.}

\KEYWORDS{simulation optimization, transfer learning, structured priors,
chance constraints, knowledge gradient, independent verification}

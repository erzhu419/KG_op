\section{Source-Learned Structural Prior}\label{sec:mp_model}

\subsection{Ordered policies and observable coordinates}

Let $h:[0,1]\to[0,1]$ be an ordered policy profile.  A public candidate $x$
is represented by an eight-dimensional coordinate $\psi(x)$ constructed from
the declared trajectory and cost exposures.  This coordinate is observable
before target outcomes are collected.  It is not an encoder trained from
unobserved target trajectories.  A nominally long profile is therefore treated
as a discretized ordered rule, rather than as an arbitrary vector of unrelated
free variables.

For target task $q$, one replication returns an objective $Y^f_q(x)$ and a
constraint observation $Y^g_q(x)$.  We minimize $f_q(x)=\mathbb E Y^f_q(x)$
under the chance constraint
\begin{equation}
 \Pr\{Y^g_q(x)\le 0\}\ge 1-\alpha.
 \label{eq:mp_chance}
\end{equation}
The environment's cumulative risk is represented by $\eta_q(x)=\log
\operatorname{Var}(Y^g_q(x))$.  The risk head describes total log variance; it
does not claim to identify separate physical common and local shocks.

\subsection{Finite function model}

Each source head $t\in\{f,g,\eta\}$ uses the declared finite dictionary
$\{\phi_k(\psi)\}_{k=1}^{68}$:
\begin{equation}
 H_{q,t}(x)=b_{q,t}+\sum_{k=1}^{68}\beta_{q,t,k}\phi_k(\psi(x))+r_{q,t}(x).
 \label{eq:mp_head}
\end{equation}
The dictionary is centered and scaled using only the public candidate bank.
Source tasks estimate which functions are useful and the cross-task coefficient
variation.  The target coefficient mean for non-intercept functions is zero;
source information enters through the covariance and the structural support.
The residual $r_{q,t}$ is epistemic model error and is kept separate from
environmental risk.

The target arms differ in the declared source information: a source-selected
support, the same-source full dictionary, or a target-only full dictionary.
A paid target observation $y$ updates the Gaussian function posterior,
\begin{equation}
 \theta_j^+ = \theta_j^-+K_j(y-\hat y_j),
 \label{eq:mp_update}
\end{equation}
where the observation noise is recorded separately for each head.  The
physical-risk head additionally maintains a finite factor-family particle
mixture; its weights are updated by the exact Gaussian log-sample-variance
likelihood.  This separates mean-structure correction from risk-family
reweighting and leaves negative transfer visible in the paired controls.

\subsection{What the prior does not claim}

The coordinate is declared, the dictionary is finite and non-orthogonal, and
the risk head models total variance.  Consequently, the present method does
not establish learned trajectory representations, separate factor recovery,
stable deployment superiority, or optimization over general independent
high-dimensional variables.  Those are distinct research questions rather
than implicit consequences of \eqref{eq:mp_head}.

# Strong source-prior claim: current gap and frozen next step

The independent v2 block failed one specific claim: the source-selected structure did not reproduce its development advantage over the same-source full dictionary. The means were `0.442743` and `0.451599`, respectively; the paired contrast was `-0.008856`, with two positive, two negative, and four tied target blocks. The verifier produced zero false certificates, so this is negative evidence about decision transfer, not a verifier bug. The largest loss came from target law seed `95800` (`0.136247` versus `0.214026`).

The generic target-only RBF-KG control is also higher on v2 (`0.459121`). This rules out the interpretation that the source-selected structure is already a generic constrained-KG improvement. The result therefore narrows the claim to finite, representation-conditional early prediction and development-block attribution.

To support a stronger paper claim, the project needs one new preregistered confirmation study, not another post hoc rerun of v2. The frozen entry point is `performance/meta_prior_universal_deployment_confirmation_v1.json`. It requires the same four arms, a nonempty feasible candidate bank, independent target blocks in at least three registered task families, a target-only RBF-KG baseline, paired confidence intervals, source and target cost accounting, and an independent verifier. The family contracts must be frozen before target outcomes are opened.

Two current blockers are now measured. The full 64-profile Energy library has zero audit-feasible candidates for DK_1 under the current 2018/1000-hour/alpha=0.05 contract, even though every candidate has a negative average constraint; the window-level reliability rule rejects them all. The Energy preflight is recorded in `paper_artifacts/meta_prior_universal_energy_preflight_v1.json`. Queue has 6--9 feasible candidates in the fixed 64-point bank, but its feasible objective span is only 0.0028--0.0229, below the frozen 0.04 separation gate. A fixed 5x5x5 constant grid does not repair the span (0.0017--0.0224); both results are recorded in `paper_artifacts/meta_prior_universal_queue_preflight_v1.json` and `paper_artifacts/meta_prior_universal_queue_grid_preflight_v1.json`. These are contract failures, not method comparisons. Energy needs a newly declared candidate/reliability contract and three target response heads. Queue needs a scale-aware separation rule justified by verification resolution and declared before any target outcomes, or a redesigned problem contract. A FactorShock synthetic alternative has enough feasible candidates on some, but not all, fixed geometry tasks; it also needs a frozen candidate/task contract before use.

The success gate is deliberately stronger than the v2 development result: a strictly positive pooled paired interval against target-only RBF-KG, nonnegative point estimates in every registered family, no material certification loss, zero false certificates, and a positive contrast against same-source full dictionary. If this gate fails, the manuscript must retain bounded wording and the negative v2 result; it must not use “stable”, “universal”, or equivalent source-prior superiority language.

Until those family contracts exist, the correct next action is contract construction and outcome-free feasibility auditing. Running more seeds on the existing Inventory contract would improve precision for one family but would not establish the requested cross-family claim.

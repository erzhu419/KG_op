Please consider “Source-Scored Structural Initial Designs for Chance-Constrained Optimization of Ordered Policy Profiles” as a Regular Manuscript in the Simulation area of Operations Research.

The paper studies the selection of a small initial simulation design when a high-dimensional decision vector discretizes an ordered policy. Replicated historical simulations rank a finite profile library; geometric selection chooses ten starting policies, and an independent terminal verifier controls false deployment. The analysis links grid-stable structural coordinates, finite-library coverage and conditional target safety, with explicit accounting for historical, target-search and verification calls.

The empirical study reports where source scoring helps and where it fails. Supplementary comparisons against matched standardized maximin and a greedy portfolio retain an aggregate certification advantage on the declared synthetic task mixture. The same comparisons use previously evaluated task seeds and are identified as supplementary analyses. A region-held-out electricity study and equal single-target cost comparisons retain negative results. We include an electronic companion and a compact anonymous code-and-data attachment supporting statistical replay and regeneration of the manuscript figures and tables.

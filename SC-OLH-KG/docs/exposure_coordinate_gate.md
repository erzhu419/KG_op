# Observable Exposure Coordinate Gate

> Status: completed and rejected by the preregistered gate. The successor is
> `observable_state_coordinate_v2.md`; no V1 challenger was promoted.

## Motivation

The previous policy-profile boundary coordinate failed its paired gate. It
worsened held-out constraint-mean error on Inventory and Queue, did not restore
FactorShock scale-4 support, and produced no posterior certificate. That result
rejects the policy-profile implementation; it does not establish that one
coordinate must explain both conditional mean and cumulative variance.

The next model starts from one source-learned, target-observable exposure
`e(x)`, then uses independent heads:

```text
phi_mu(x) = h_mu(e(x))
psi_v(x)  = h_v(e(x))

margin(x) = mu_g(phi_mu(x))
          + sqrt(beta_g) s_g(phi_mu(x))
          + z_alpha sqrt(v_C_plus(psi_v(x)))
          - tau.
```

The source-learned exposure is generated by the frozen meta-prior descriptor,
PCA, and soft regime map. It does not call a held-out target risk provider.
The target-specific provider exposure is retained only as a structure-aware
upper bound.

## Causal Tracks

All cells use the same frozen source archive, the same frozen source-informed
`n0=10`, the same target seed, and the same template-free post-run policy pool.
The default offline gate has `N=n0=10`, so no sequential target query occurs.

| Track | Mean input | Rank | Role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `latent_control` | normalized policy profile | 2 | oracle-free control |
| `profile_phi_r2` | normalized policy profile | 2 | rejected V1 control |
| `learned_exposure_phi_r2/r4/r8` | frozen source-learned exposure | 2/4/8 | oracle-free challengers |
| `provider_exposure_phi_r2/r4/r8` | target provider exposure | 2/4/8 | privileged upper bounds only |

The audit pool disables source boundary templates. Consequently its raw
policies are identical across all tracks for a fixed seed. Target truth is read
only after the recommendation is frozen and cannot alter an observation or
decision.

## Preregistered Gate

The matrix covers FactorShock scale 0, FactorShock scale 4, Inventory, and
Queue, with five seeds per cell at `d=1000`.

An oracle-free learned-exposure rank advances only if:

1. Its constraint-mean rank correlation is nonworse than `latent_control`
   within `0.02` in at least three of four scenarios.
2. Its median absolute error is no larger than
   `max(2 * control_MAE, control_MAE + 0.05)` in every scenario.
3. The common raw pool contains a true-feasible FactorShock scale-4 policy in
   at least four of five seeds.
4. Replacing fitted mean and variance with their post-run oracle counterparts
   yields at least one certifiable policy in at least three of four scenarios.
5. Every provider-exposure cell remains labeled non-oracle-free.

Among passing ranks, select the highest median held-out mean-rank correlation;
ties choose the smaller rank. Only this selected rank may enter a sequential
gate.

The sequential gate fixes that rank and compares `latent_control`,
`exposure_mean_only`, and `exposure_mean_proposal` at `N=20,n0=10`. Promotion
requires complete paired cells, mean-rank noninferiority in at least three of
four scenarios, no scenario-level feasibility loss, at least one feasibility
gain, nonzero FactorShock scale-4 proposal support, at least one sound
certificate, no false certificate, and no adaptive loss.

## Failure Interpretation

- Learned exposure loses while provider exposure wins: the source-learned
  observable exposure is insufficient or misaligned.
- Both learned and provider exposure lose: the independent mean head or its
  target calibration is the bottleneck.
- Both rank well but oracle-both certification is empty: the benchmark has
  insufficient safety depth under the current epistemic radius.
- The common pool lacks FactorShock scale-4 support: candidate support, not
  posterior ranking, is the binding failure.
- Offline gate passes but sequential gate fails: candidate selection or online
  adaptation, rather than coordinate sufficiency, is responsible.

No failed track is promoted or pushed as a new baseline.

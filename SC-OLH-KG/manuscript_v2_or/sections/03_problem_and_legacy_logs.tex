\section{Problem Contract and Debiased Legacy-Trajectory Meta-Prior}\label{sec:prior}

\subsection{Roster-Invariant Decision Contract and Public Nominal Rollout}

Consider $M_{\mathrm{src}}$ historical source tasks $m \in \{1,\dots,M_{\mathrm{src}}\}$ and a target task $t$. Task $k \in \{1,\dots,M_{\mathrm{src}}\} \cup \{t\}$ has $C_k \in \{1, 2\}$ interleaved operational control channels over horizon/network size $H_k$, giving policy space $\cX_k = [0,1]^{d_k}$ with $d_k = C_k H_k$ (or $d_k$ variable signal phases), where $d_m \neq d_t$ across horizons or subnetworks. Evaluating $x \in \cX_k$ for $r \ge 1$ replications under private exogenous law $\mathbb{Q}_k$ yields i.i.d.\ realizations
\begin{equation}\label{eq:obs_model}
  Y_{k,f,\ell}(x) = J_k(x) + \varepsilon_{k,f,\ell}(x),
  \qquad
  Y_{k,g,\ell}(x) = g_k(x) + \varepsilon_{k,g,\ell}(x)
  \qquad (\ell = 1,\dots,r),
\end{equation}
with $\E[\varepsilon_{k,c,\ell}(x)] = 0$, policy-dependent variances $v_{k,c}(x) = \Var(Y_{k,c,\ell}(x))$ ($c \in \{f, g\}$), and true violation probability $p_k(x) = \Prob_{\mathbb{Q}_k}(Y_{k,g,\ell}(x) > 0)$. The target optimization problem is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:main_ccp}
  \min_{x \in \cX_t} \; J_t(x)
  \qquad \text{subject to} \qquad p_t(x) \le \alpha.
\end{equation}
Each target task $t$ specifies a public domain status-quo anchor $x_t^{\mathrm{sq}} \in \cX_t$, search call budget $B_{\mathrm{search}} = (n_0 + N) m_{\mathrm{batch}}$, and independent verification cap $B_{\mathrm{ver}} = \MaxVerifCalls$. A method outputs either a certified deployment ($\mathrm{state} = \texttt{certified\_deployment}$, $\hat{x}_t \in \cX_t$) or a strict abstention ($\mathrm{state} = \texttt{abstention}$, $\hat{x}_t = \varnothing$), incurring roster-invariant status-quo loss
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sq_loss}
  \mathcal{L}_t(\mathrm{state}, \hat{x}_t) \coloneqq
  \begin{cases}
    J_t(\hat{x}_t), & \text{if } \mathrm{state} = \texttt{certified\_deployment}, \\
    J_t(x_t^{\mathrm{sq}}), & \text{if } \mathrm{state} = \texttt{abstention},
  \end{cases} .
\end{equation}
Let $C$ be certification and let $X_t^{\mathrm{op}}=\hat x_t$ on $C$ and $X_t^{\mathrm{op}}=x_t^{\mathrm{sq}}$ otherwise. Given the target task and frozen search history $H$, the unsafe-operation probability under this rule is
\begin{align}\label{eq:unsafe_operation}
\Prob(p_t(X_t^{\mathrm{op}})>\alpha\mid H)
={}&\Prob(C,\ p_t(\hat x_t)>\alpha\mid H)\notag\\
&+\mathbb{I}\{p_t(x_t^{\mathrm{sq}})>\alpha\}\Prob(C^c\mid H).
\end{align}
When $p_t$ is evaluated under the verification law, Theorem~\ref{thm:vkg_safety} bounds the first term by $\delta$. With a feasible anchor under that law, the same bound applies to unsafe operation. Otherwise the fallback probability enters in full. In both new panels we audit the operating policy on either branch and report the resulting safety classes. The matched panel also reports average violation rates. We also report delay-penalized loss $\mathcal{L}_{t,\eta}(\mathrm{state}, \hat{x}_t) \coloneqq J_t(\hat{x}_t)\mathbb{I}\{\text{certified}\} + (1 + \eta)J_t(x_t^{\mathrm{sq}})\mathbb{I}\{\text{abstained}\}$ ($\eta = 0.10$ as a sensitivity scenario) and assess the finite-sample safety requirement $\Prob(\mathrm{state} = \texttt{certified\_deployment} \text{ and } p_t(\hat{x}_t) > \alpha) \le \delta$.

\paragraph{Public Nominal Rollout ($\psi \in \R^{\PsiDim}$).}
Prior to paid simulation on task $k$, the algorithm observes only \emph{public planning inputs} $\cI_k^{\mathrm{pub}}$ (published demand/arrival forecasts, storage ratings, signal phase bounds)—never private shock laws $\mathbb{Q}_k$ or realized forecast errors. Deterministic rollout of $x \in [0,1]^{d_k}$ under $\cI_k^{\mathrm{pub}}$ produces a nominal trajectory $\cT_k^{\mathrm{nom}}(x)$ and fixed-dimensional state-risk summary
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nominal_exposure}
  x \in [0,1]^{d_k} \;\longmapsto\; \cT_k^{\mathrm{nom}}(x) \;\longmapsto\;
  \mathcal{E}_k(x) = \big(\psi(x),\, A_{\mathrm{local}}(x),\, N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x)\big) \in \R^{\PsiDim} \times \R_+^{4} \times \R_+^{3},
\end{equation}
where $\psi(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$ summarizes nominal utilization, buffer headroom, throughput, and regime deficits; $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x) \in \R_+^4$ captures RMS local volatility exposures; and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x) \in \R_+^3$ measures off-peak, shoulder, and peak regime occupancies. Automated unit tests across all four benchmark families verify bit-exact invariance ($\|\Delta\psi\|_\infty = 0$) when private shock parameters or historical error arrays are overwritten. Because $d_k \gg \PsiDim$, $x \mapsto \psi(x)$ is a behavioral compression rather than a bijection; we quantify both its smooth explainability and many-to-one collision sensitivity in Section~\ref{sec:p1_diag}.

\paragraph{Within-Policy Sufficient Statistics and Heterogeneous ``Dark-Data'' Archives.}
For every policy evaluation of $r \ge 1$ replications, we store $\bar{Y}_{k,c}(x) = r^{-1}\sum_{\ell=1}^r Y_{k,c,\ell}(x)$, within-policy sum of squares $S_{k,c}^2(x) = \sum_{\ell=1}^r (Y_{k,c,\ell}(x) - \bar{Y}_{k,c}(x))^2$ (with $S_{k,c}^2(x) \equiv 0$ whenever $r = 1$), and violation count $V_k(x) = \sum_{\ell=1}^r \mathbb{I}\{Y_{k,g,\ell}(x) > 0\}$, merging repeat visits via exact parallel variance formulas. Notably, real enterprise archives rarely consist of balanced multi-replication designs: historical tasks may mix single-replication screening evaluations ($r_a = 1$, $S_{c,a}^2 = 0$), $3.7\times$ unequal per-task simulation budgets, incomplete solver subsets per task, and mismatched historical reliability targets $\alpha_m \neq \alpha_t$. Our offline prior extraction handles both clean and messy archives without discarding $r_a = 1$ mean information or corrupting noise estimates.

\subsection{Occupancy-Balanced Orthogonal Meta-Prior and Multi-Channel DCT Templates}

Given an offline legacy archive $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{src}} = \{(m_a, x_a, r_a, \bar{Y}_{f,a}, S_{f,a}^2, \bar{Y}_{g,a}, S_{g,a}^2, \mathcal{E}(x_a))\}_{a=1}^{N_{\mathrm{src}}}$ generated across $M_{\mathrm{src}}$ source tasks by adaptive legacy solvers, we extract the meta-prior in four closed-form steps:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Inverse-Occupancy Manifold Balancing.} Standardizing $\tilde{\psi}_a = \operatorname{diag}(\hat{\sigma}_\psi)^{-1}(\psi_a - \hat{\mu}_\psi)$ and forming Gaussian kernel affinities $W_{ab} = \exp(-\|\tilde{\psi}_a - \tilde{\psi}_b\|_2^2 / (2 h_q^2))$ with empirical density $\hat{q}_a = N_{\mathrm{src}}^{-1}\sum_{b=1}^{N_{\mathrm{src}}} W_{ab}$, we compute clipped inverse-occupancy weights ($\bar{w} = 6$)
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:inv_occ_weights}
    w_a = \frac{\min\{\bar{w},\; \operatorname{med}(\hat{q}^{-1})^{-1}\hat{q}_a^{-1}\}}{\sum_{b=1}^{N_{\mathrm{src}}} \min\{\bar{w},\; \operatorname{med}(\hat{q}^{-1})^{-1}\hat{q}_b^{-1}\}},
    \qquad \hat{\nu}_w = \sum_{a=1}^{N_{\mathrm{src}}} w_a \delta_{\tilde{\psi}_a},
  \end{equation}
  downweighting dense solver clusters in empirical inner products without claiming exact population density recovery.

  \item \textbf{Smooth Dictionary \& Graph-Laplacian Low-Pass Filtering.} Over $\tilde{\psi} \in \R^{\PsiDim}$, a $\RawDictDim$-feature dictionary $\varphi(\tilde{\psi})$ combines linear terms $\tilde{\psi}_j$, quadratic Legendre polynomials $(\tilde{\psi}_j^2 - 1)/\sqrt{2}$, bounded sine modes $\sin(\frac{\pi}{2}\operatorname{clip}(\tilde{\psi}_j/2.5, -1, 1))$, and structured cross-terms $\tilde{\psi}_j \tilde{\psi}_k$. Using the normalized graph Laplacian $L_{\mathrm{sym}} = I - D^{-1/2} W D^{-1/2}$, features above the $\rho_{\mathrm{lp}} = 0.75$ quantile of Dirichlet roughness $\bar{\varphi}_j^\top L_{\mathrm{sym}} \bar{\varphi}_j / (\|\bar{\varphi}_j\|_2^2 + \epsilon_0)$ are filtered out ($39$ of $\RawDictDim$ survive).

  \item \textbf{Gram Whitening, Finite-Source Empirical Bayes, \& Bayesian Pruned-Mode Features.} Centering $\mathring{\varphi} = \varphi_{\mathrm{lp}} - \E_{\hat{\nu}_w}[\varphi_{\mathrm{lp}}]$ and eigendecomposing $G_w = \sum_a w_a \mathring{\varphi}_a \mathring{\varphi}_a^\top = U\Lambda U^\top$, whitening $Q_{\mathrm{orth}} = \Lambda_+^{-1/2}U_+^\top$ ($\Lambda_+ > 10^{-5}\lambda_{\max}$) yields $\hat{\nu}_w$-orthonormal coordinates $\tilde{\varphi}(\psi) = Q_{\mathrm{orth}}\mathring{\varphi}(\tilde{\psi}) \in \R^{M_+}$. Per-task precision-weighted ridge-GLS estimates $(\hat{\theta}_{m,f}, \hat{\theta}_{m,g})$ (weighting each record by $w_a r_a$, thereby incorporating both $r_a=1$ and $r_a \ge 2$ logs) rank coordinates via $\mathcal{I}_k = \sum_{c \in \{f,g\}} \omega_c \big(\bar{\theta}_{k,c}^2 / (\Var_m(\hat{\theta}_{m,k,c}) + \epsilon_0) + \tfrac{1}{2}\E_m[\hat{\theta}_{m,k,c}^2]\big)$, retaining $|S_{\mathrm{act}}| = \ActiveRank$ active basis functions $\tilde{\phi}(x) = [1, \tilde{\varphi}_{S_{\mathrm{act}}}(\psi(x))^\top]^\top \in \R^{1+\ActiveRank}$. Across the $M_{\mathrm{src}}$ source tasks, we set $\mu_{0,c} = \bar{\theta}_{S_{\mathrm{act}},c}$ and inflate the shrinkage-stabilized between-task covariance by $(1 + 1/M_{\mathrm{src}})$ to capture finite-source meta-mean estimation uncertainty:
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:finite_source_cov}
    \Sigma_{0,c} = \left(1 + \frac{1}{M_{\mathrm{src}}}\right)\Big(0.85\,\widehat{\operatorname{Cov}}_m(\hat{\theta}_{m,S_{\mathrm{act}},c}) + 0.15\,\operatorname{diag}_{\ge 10^{-3}}\!\big(\widehat{\operatorname{Cov}}_m\big)\Big) + 10^{-4} I_{1+\ActiveRank}.
  \end{equation}
  Rather than adding an unconditioned post-hoc penalty that never shrinks with target data, pruned coordinates $k \in S_{\mathrm{act}}^c$ define energy-weighted feature vectors $w_{\perp,c}(x) = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\tilde{\varphi}_{S_{\mathrm{act}}^c}(\psi(x)) \odot \sqrt{\hat{e}_{\perp,c}}$ (where $\hat{e}_{\perp,f}, \hat{e}_{\perp,g}$ are the $0.80$ and $0.85$ quantiles of $\{\hat{\theta}_{m,k,c}^2\}_{m=1}^{M_{\mathrm{src}}}$), included in the online GP covariance. With covariance parameters held fixed, conditioning reduces uncertainty in the pruned-mode component at evaluated policies.

  \item \textbf{Source HVD Hyperprior \& Single-Rep-Safe DCT Templates.} Forming the $14$-feature nonnegative variance vector $u_{\mathrm{HVD}}(x) = [1, A_{\mathrm{local}}(x)^{\odot 2}, \{\tfrac{1}{2}(N_i(x)+N_j(x))^2\}_{i \le j}, N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x)]^\top \in \R_+^{14}$, degrees-of-freedom-weighted NNLS is fitted \emph{exclusively} on multi-replication records $\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rep}} = \{a : r_a \ge 2\}$ ($N_{\mathrm{src}}^{\mathrm{rep}} = |\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rep}}|$), never mistaking $S_{c,a}^2 = 0$ on $r_a = 1$ logs for zero noise; the source variance fit uses scale adjustments based on $\operatorname{med}_{a \in \mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{rep}}}(S_{c,a}^2/(r_a - 1))$ (falling back to GLS residual dispersion only if $N_{\mathrm{src}}^{\mathrm{rep}} < 6$). Finally, elite feasible source policies—screened via $\bar{Y}_{g,a} + 1.645\hat{s}_{\mathrm{eff},a} \le 0$ using HVD-imputed dispersion $\hat{s}_{\mathrm{eff},a}$ whenever $r_a = 1$—are compressed per control channel ($C_k \in \{1, 2\}$) via orthonormal DCT-II into $K_{\mathrm{dct}} = 12$ spectral modes, providing warm starts for the target horizons and channel layouts in these experiments.
\end{enumerate}


For fixed basis and hyperparameters, the model induces a prior over response functions within each business family. Its coefficient component is $h_c(x)=\tilde\phi(x)^\top b_c$ with $b_c\sim\mathcal N(\mu_{0,c},\Sigma_{0,c})$. The resulting relation between policies is $\operatorname{Cov}(h_c(x),h_c(x'))=\tilde\phi(x)^\top\Sigma_{0,c}\tilde\phi(x')$. Replacing $\Sigma_{0,c}$ by its diagonal retains the marginal prior variance of each coefficient and removes the cross-mode terms. The corresponding change to the variance of a policy difference between $u$ and $v$ is $d_{uv}^\top[\Sigma_{0,c}-\operatorname{diag}(\Sigma_{0,c})]d_{uv}$, where $d_{uv}=\tilde\phi(u)-\tilde\phi(v)$. The residual GP and the inactive-mode component are specified separately in the target model. All reported optimization decisions are searches over finite vectors $x$ with a shared finite feature representation across horizons or subnetworks.

\section{Managerial Insights and Concluding Remarks}\label{sec:conclusion}

Operational simulation models—whether tracking multi-echelon inventories, service queues, grid storage assets, or urban signalized corridors—are rarely used once and discarded; organizations repeatedly optimize related system variants over rolling horizons. Yet historical optimization logs generated by legacy heuristics (\texttt{SPSA}, \texttt{CMA-ES}, \texttt{NSGA-II}, or practitioner rules) have largely remained stranded ``dark data'' because they are dimension-mismatched ($d_s \neq d_t$), heavily skewed toward elite basins, and prone to inducing catastrophic variance blow-up when naively conditioned under source--target shift.

\textbf{TLS-OLH-KG} demonstrates that all three barriers admit clean, closed-form operational remedies:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \emph{Transfer through public physics, not raw coordinates.} Projecting policies onto nominal trajectory exposures $\psi(\cT^{\mathrm{nom}}(x))$ and two-channel low-frequency DCT modes makes historical experience portable across planning horizons and network sizes without leaking private outcomes.
  \item \emph{Debias offline logs and decouple online variance from mean residuals.} Inverse-occupancy weighting restores functional orthogonality on skewed legacy trajectories, while estimating HVD variance strictly from within-policy replication dispersion $S^2(x)$—paired with marginal-likelihood tempering—eliminates mean-shift-to-aleatoric leakage without sacrificing multi-step adaptability.
  \item \emph{Pair fast lookahead VOI with distribution-free clean-up verification.} Closed-form rank-one updates make joint $(f,g)$ Verification-Aware Knowledge Gradient tractable in milliseconds per step, while a feasible-aware three-slot independent binomial/noncentral-$t$ gate guarantees finite-sample deployment safety ($\mathrm{FWER} \le \delta$) even under skewed discrete-event dynamics.
\end{enumerate}

Future work may extend this framework to multi-objective Pareto frontier certification, dynamic contextual adaptation under nonstationary streaming arrivals, and distributed batch Knowledge Gradient allocation across parallel simulation workers.

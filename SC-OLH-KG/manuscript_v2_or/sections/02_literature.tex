\section{Literature Positioning}\label{sec:lit}

Our work bridges four streams of research in simulation optimization and statistical machine learning.

\paragraph{Knowledge Gradient and Heteroscedastic Simulation Metamodeling.}
The Knowledge Gradient (KG) policy maximizes the expected single-step value of information under correlated normal beliefs \citep{frazier2008knowledge,frazier2009correlated} and continuous Gaussian process (GP) surrogates \citep{scott2011correlated}. For stochastic simulations with state-dependent variance, Stochastic Kriging \citep{ankenman2010stochastic} and heteroscedastic GPs \citep[\texttt{hetGP};][]{binois2018practical,binois2019replication} separate extrinsic surface uncertainty from intrinsic Monte Carlo noise to balance exploration against replication; \citet{makarova2021risk} incorporate input-dependent noise into risk-averse Bayesian optimization. \textbf{GP-FTA} extends heteroscedastic metamodeling to cross-horizon transfer by modeling variance on nonnegative physical regime exposures $(A_{\mathrm{local}}(x), N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x))$ fitted strictly to within-policy dispersion $S^2(x)/(r-1)$, preventing prior mean-shift leakage under regime shifts.

In constrained simulation optimization, \citet{ungredda2024bayesian} weight expected objective improvement by posterior probability of feasibility $\Prob(g(x) \le 0)$ and score queries by one-step lookahead on that product. \textbf{GP-FTA} generalizes decision-focused lookahead from asymptotic feasibility to \emph{finite-sample clean-up certification}: first by weighting savings with the exact binomial gate operating characteristic $C_{K_0,k^\star}(p)$ (which assigns $0.0221$ rather than $0.50$ at the $\alpha=0.05$ boundary when $K_0=\SlotZeroK$), and second—through \textbf{EC-KG}—by valuing the entire post-query three-slot guarded shortlist nominated from the dynamically expanding eligible set $\mathcal{D}_n \cup \{q\}$.

\paragraph{High-Dimensional and Trajectory-Summary Bayesian Optimization.}
Cold-start high-dimensional Bayesian optimization relies on local trust regions \citep[\texttt{TuRBO} and \texttt{SCBO};][]{eriksson2019scalable,eriksson2021scalable}, sparse axis-aligned subspace priors \citep[\texttt{SAASBO};][]{eriksson2021high}, or additive kernels \citep{kandasamy2015high,lu2022additive}. In sequential control, \citet{wilson2014using} show that kernels defined over state-action trajectories capture behavioral similarity missed by Euclidean parameter distance. Because mapping $x \in [0,1]^{d_t}$ onto a $\PsiDim$-dimensional public nominal rollout summary $\psi(x)$ is necessarily many-to-one ($d_t \gg \PsiDim$), \textbf{GP-FTA} neither treats $\psi(x)$ as an injective coordinate chart nor discards raw policy geometry: multi-channel Discrete Cosine Transform (DCT) templates preserve temporal and spatial phase ordering in candidate proposals, while a Mat\'ern-5/2 residual GP over $\psi(x)$ absorbs task-specific nonlinearities orthogonal to the source subspace. To isolate prior transfer from representation effects, our experiments explicitly benchmark same-representation adaptations (\texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}, \texttt{SCBO-on-Psi}, and \texttt{RGPE-on-Psi}) alongside raw-space baselines.

\paragraph{Meta-Learning Bayesian Optimization, Empirical Bayes, and Stein--Mallows Shrinkage.}
Meta-learning BO transfers knowledge across related tasks via ranking-weighted GP ensembles \citep[\texttt{RGPE};][]{feurer2018scalable}, empirical-Bayes multi-task GP pretraining \citep[\texttt{HyperBO};][]{wang2024pretrained}, or PAC-Bayesian hyperpriors \citep[\texttt{PACOH};][]{rothfuss2021pacoh}. Operational simulation archives depart from standard meta-BO assumptions because dimensions vary ($d_s \neq d_t$), adaptive solvers induce severe occupancy skew, enterprise logs mix $r_a = 1$ and $r_a \ge 2$ evaluations under heterogeneous historical risk tolerances $\alpha_m \neq \alpha_t$, and misspecified hierarchical priors degrade performance unless tempered and released \citep{bissiri2016general,grunwald2017inconsistency,rasmussen2006gaussian}. By projecting centered target observations onto the whitened target Fisher eigenspace, \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} embeds classical Stein--Mallows unbiased risk estimation \citep{mallows1973some,efron1973stein,stein1981estimation} directly inside online GP prior shrinkage and release, achieving uniform excess-risk protection across arbitrary prior--target shifts.

\paragraph{Chance-Constrained Simulation Optimization and Two-Stage Clean-Up.}
Chance-constrained simulation optimization methods combine Monte Carlo Feasibility tests with sequential surrogates \citep{hong2011sequential,letham2019constrained}, while chance-constrained ranking and selection guarantees feasibility and optimality over finite sets under explicit indifference-zone sampling \citep{hong2015chance}. Following the clean-up paradigm of \citet{boesel2003using}, \textbf{GP-FTA} freezes a compact shortlist of evaluated policies after search and tests them on fresh simulation replications via exact Clopper--Pearson / binomial gates \citep{clopper1934use} with asymmetric familywise error spending, separating distribution-free false-certification control from surrogate modeling assumptions.

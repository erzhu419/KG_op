\section{Literature Positioning}\label{sec:lit}

Our work connects four research streams across simulation optimization, ranking and selection, and statistical transfer learning.

\paragraph{Knowledge Gradient, Sampled-Design Restriction, and Heteroscedastic Metamodeling.}
The Knowledge Gradient (KG) policy maximizes single-step value of information under correlated normal beliefs \citep{frazier2008knowledge,frazier2009correlated} and continuous Gaussian process (GP) surrogates \citep{scott2011correlated}. Crucially, \citet{scott2011correlated} distinguish unrestricted continuous implementation from \emph{Knowledge Gradient for Continuous Parameters} (\texttt{KGCP}), which restricts terminal implementation to the finite set of designs $\mathcal{D}_n \cup \{q\}$ actually evaluated during search; under \texttt{KGCP}, querying an unevaluated design naturally combines posterior belief updates over $\mathcal{D}_n$ with admitting $q$ into the implementable set. Our shortlist-menu proxy (\textbf{EC-KG}) builds directly on this sampled-design principle, extending it from unconstrained scalar posterior-mean maximization to a correlated three-slot exact-binomial clean-up cascade. For simulations with state-dependent noise, Stochastic Kriging \citep{ankenman2010stochastic} and heteroscedastic GPs \citep{binois2018practical,binois2019replication,makarova2021risk} separate extrinsic surface uncertainty from intrinsic Monte Carlo variance; \textbf{GP-FTA} adapts heteroscedastic regression to shifted transfer settings by fitting nonnegative volatility features strictly to centered within-policy dispersion $S^2(x)/(r-1)$, preventing prior mean mismatch from inflating noise estimates.

In constrained Bayesian optimization, standard acquisitions weight expected improvement by marginal feasibility probability \citep{gelbart2014bayesian,gardner2014bayesian,eriksson2021scalable}. Under observation noise, \citet{letham2019constrained} and \citet[Section~5]{ungredda2024bayesian} analyze noisy expected improvement (\texttt{NEI}, which integrates over latent objective and constraint realizations across observed designs to recompute the feasible incumbent per draw) alongside constrained KG (\texttt{cKG}) followed by a final \texttt{NEI} step when deployment is restricted to sampled designs. Our setting differs in its terminal deployment rule: rather than deploying by posterior expectation alone, terminal clean-up executes a finite-sample, objective-guarded three-slot binomial test where a policy on the $\alpha = 0.05$ boundary passes a $K_0 = \SlotZeroK$, $k^\star = 1$ gate with probability $0.0221$ rather than $0.50$, and expected loss depends jointly on primary gate power, incumbent guarding, and backup rescue. We benchmark \textbf{EC-KG} against both deterministic posterior-mean plug-in constrained EI (\texttt{PlugIn-cEI}) and latent-integrated \texttt{LatentNEI} in closed loop and on frozen states.

\paragraph{Stochastic Constrained Ranking \& Selection, Feasibility Determination, and Clean-Up.}
When choosing among finitely many simulated alternatives, two-stage screening and post-search ``clean-up'' decouple heuristic search from rigorous statistical selection \citep{koenig1985procedure,nelson2001simple,boesel2003using}, analogous to out-of-sample solution-quality bounding in stochastic programming \citep{mak1999monte,bayraksan2006assessing}. For stochastic constraints on finite sets, feasibility determination \citep{szechtman2008feasibility,batur2010finding}, fully sequential constrained comparison \citep{andradottir2010fully,healey2014selection}, optimal constrained computing budget allocation \citep[\texttt{OCBA-CO} and \texttt{SCORE};][]{lee2012approximate,hunter2013optimal,pasupathy2015stochastically}, and chance-constrained selection of the best \citep{hong2015chance} allocate replications across candidates under indifference-zone or asymptotic normality assumptions; \citet{hong2011sequential} study sequential Monte Carlo approximations for continuous joint chance constraints, and \citet{eckman2023diagnostic} standardize diagnostic evaluation for simulation-optimization solvers. Following \citet{boesel2003using}, we freeze a compact three-slot shortlist after continuous search and test it on fresh draws via exact Clopper--Pearson binomial gates \citep{clopper1934use}, showing further that classical sequential binomial curtailment \citep{wald1945sequential,batur2010finding} preserves the exact sample-path pass indicator while cutting verification calls on infeasible candidates.

\paragraph{Control Summaries, Multi-Source KG, and Empirical-Bayes Meta-Learning.}
High-dimensional cold-start BO relies on trust regions \citep{eriksson2019scalable,eriksson2021scalable}, sparse axis-aligned priors \citep{eriksson2021high}, or additive kernels \citep{kandasamy2015high,lu2022additive}. In sequential control, \citet{wilson2014using} estimate behavior-based trajectory kernels for unexecuted policies via importance sampling over logged stochastic trajectories on a fixed horizon; by contrast, \textbf{GP-FTA} uses deterministic nominal rollouts driven solely by public planning inputs $\cI_k^{\mathrm{pub}}$ to form a horizon-invariant summary $\psi(x)$ at zero stochastic rollout cost. Across related tasks, \citet{poloczek2017multi} develop warm-start multi-information-source KG combining historical task GPs with a target discrepancy process, while multi-task GP coregionalization \citep{swersky2013multi}, adaptive Bayesian linear regression on shared basis dictionaries \citep[\texttt{ABLR};][]{perrone2018scalable}, ranking-weighted GP ensembles \citep[\texttt{RGPE};][]{feurer2018scalable}, empirical-Bayes pretraining \citep[\texttt{HyperBO};][]{wang2024pretrained}, and PAC-Bayesian hyperpriors \citep{rothfuss2021pacoh} transfer structure over shared input spaces. Operational simulation archives, however, combine adaptive solver occupancy skew, mixed $r_a = 1$ and $r_a \ge 2$ logs, and target shifts that penalize unshrunk priors \citep{bissiri2016general,grunwald2017inconsistency,rasmussen2006gaussian}. Over a prescribed low-order dictionary on $\psi(x)$, we pair occupancy-balanced spectral basis extraction with an online Fisher-subspace Stein--Mallows (\texttt{SURE}) shrinkage rule grounded in classical minimax shrinkage \citep{james1961estimation,baranchik1970family,mallows1973some,efron1973stein,stein1981estimation}, and benchmark directly against same-$\psi$ \texttt{RGPE}, \texttt{ABLR}, and \citet{poloczek2017multi}-style warm-start multi-task GP KG.

\section{Literature Positioning}\label{sec:lit}

Our work bridges four streams of research across operations research and statistical machine learning.

\paragraph{Knowledge Gradient and Heteroscedastic Simulation Metamodeling.}
The Knowledge Gradient (KG) policy maximizes the one-step expected value of information under correlated normal beliefs \citep{frazier2008knowledge,frazier2009correlated} and continuous GP surrogates \citep{scott2011correlated}. For stochastic simulations with input-dependent variance, Stochastic Kriging \citep{ankenman2010stochastic} and heteroscedastic GPs \citep[\texttt{hetGP};][]{binois2018practical,binois2019replication} separate extrinsic response-surface uncertainty from intrinsic Monte Carlo noise and study sequential allocation between exploring new points and replicating existing ones; \citet{makarova2021risk} incorporate input-dependent variance into risk-averse BO. However, existing heteroscedastic Kriging and KG methods start cold on each problem instance and model variance as an unstructured spatial field over raw coordinates $x \in \R^d$, requiring substantial sample budgets when $d$ is large. By contrast, we decompose cumulative trajectory variance along low-frequency state-risk exposures $(A_{\mathrm{local}}(x), N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x))$ and warm-start both mean and variance beliefs from historical legacy optimization logs.

\paragraph{High-Dimensional and Functional-Subspace Bayesian Optimization.}
Cold-start high-dimensional BO relies on structural assumptions in either input space or function space: trust-region localization \citep[\texttt{TuRBO} and \texttt{SCBO};][]{eriksson2019scalable,eriksson2021scalable}, sparse axis-aligned coordinate priors \citep[\texttt{SAASBO};][]{eriksson2021high}, and orthogonal additive functional ANOVA kernels \citep[\texttt{OAK};][]{kandasamy2015high,lu2022additive}. In dynamic policy search, \citet{wilson2014using} show that similarity kernels defined over state-action trajectories capture behavioral equivalence missed by Euclidean parameter distance. Our representation unifies trajectory-based behavioral coupling with orthogonal low-frequency functional ANOVA: smoothness, orthogonality, and sparsity are enforced on the policy-induced nominal exposure manifold $\psi(x)$ rather than raw policy coordinates, enabling dimension-invariant transfer across planning horizons $d_s \neq d_t$.

\paragraph{Meta-Learning BO, Off-Policy Selection Bias, and Tempered Posteriors.}
Meta-BO methods transfer knowledge across related tasks via ranking-weighted GP ensembles \citep[\texttt{RGPE};][]{feurer2018scalable}, empirical-Bayes pre-trained GPs \citep[\texttt{HyperBO};][]{wang2024pretrained}, or PAC-Bayesian hyperpriors \citep[\texttt{PACOH};][]{rothfuss2021pacoh}. Two gaps separate these methods from operational simulation practice. First, they assume homoscedastic, conformable input spaces and overlook the strong selection bias inherent in historical optimization logs generated by adaptive solvers \citep{spall1992multivariate,hansen2006cma,deb2002fast}. Second, Bayesian statistics has shown that misspecified linear and hierarchical models can suffer severe variance inflation and inconsistency unless tempered \citep{bissiri2016general,grunwald2017inconsistency}. Our Theorem~\ref{thm:tempering} identifies a sharp manifestation of this phenomenon in chance-constrained transfer—exponential inflation of aleatoric risk bounds due to mean-shift leakage—and resolves it via sufficient-statistic decoupling and prequential marginal-likelihood tempering.

\paragraph{Chance-Constrained Simulation Optimization and Independent Clean-Up Verification.}
Solving chance-constrained programs via simulation \citep{letham2019constrained,hong2021solving} faces acute selection bias when the same noisy evaluations guide search and certify feasibility. Two-stage ``clean-up'' procedures \citep{boesel2003using} decouple heuristic search from rigorous statistical ranking and selection. Extending this principle to chance constraints, our terminal deployment stage evaluates a frozen, feasible-aware three-slot shortlist on fresh simulation streams using either exact noncentral-$t$ tolerance bounds (under Gaussian noise) or exact distribution-free binomial certificates \citep{clopper1934use} (under skewed discrete-event dynamics), guaranteeing finite-sample familywise safety $\le \delta$ independently of surrogate misspecification.

\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}

The evidence comprises earlier development diagnostics (P1), comparisons of the preceding version (P2), and reruns on the P2 panel (U3). U3 retains the P2 source and target configurations, evaluation streams, and audit seeds, including the grid raw-tail and calibrated microscopic traffic panels. Moreover, the DK\_2 grid task was used in the P1 representation diagnostic. The archived manifests and ledgers support paired comparisons on these configurations. The panel was available during development, so the resulting comparisons are retrospective.

The frozen U3 Python runtime (SHA-256 prefix \texttt{5965482dba30}) produced $7{,}544$ main and raw-tail runs, $2{,}108$ heterogeneous-archive runs, and $144$ calibrated SUMO runs. In the U3 tables, GP-FTA-KG and GP-FTA-TS refer to these frozen runs. The KG run used nine-point quadrature for the current gate-power estimate and seven-point quadrature inside fantasies. The post-U3 implementation aligns the current reference with the fantasy integration. The ledger measures the original implementation. The separate prospective studies use the numerical correction. All runs used the recorded single-threaded cluster protocol.

\subsection{Four Dynamic Operational Simulation Families}

Targets use risk threshold $\alpha=0.05$ and familywise test budget $\delta=0.05$. Public rollouts provide $\psi(x)\in\R^{\PsiDim}$ and exposure descriptors $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x)\in\R_+^4$ and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x)\in\R_+^3$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dual-sourcing inventory.} Regular and emergency replenishment have separate control channels. Costs include holding, expedited supply, and shortages under correlated demand shocks and random regular supply yield. The six source configurations have $H_s\in\{36,48\}$ periods ($d_s=2H_s$). The sixteen target configurations have $H_t\in\{96,120\}$ periods and a $1.30\times$ shift.
  \item \textbf{Nonstationary queueing and surge diversion.} Staffing and diversion policies face overdispersed arrivals, service degradation under high load, and abandonment. The many-server setting motivates the role of utilization in the state summary \citep{halfin1981heavy}. The six source and sixteen target configurations use the same horizon ranges as inventory. The chance constraint limits the frequency of critical queue breaches.
  \item \textbf{European grid storage.} The Open Power System Data archive \citep{opsd2020timeseries} supports single-channel reserve decisions. Six source markets at $d_s\in\{168,240\}$ transfer to fourteen distinct target markets at $d_t=336$. The simulator concatenates sampled 48-hour blocks from 2017--2019 within each replication. Chronologically ordered blocks with a 24-hour isolation buffer are assigned in rotation to search, verification, and audit, and public profiles use search blocks. The independent audit block pool is distinct from the verification pool within the same period. The grid suite compares clipped and unclipped forecast errors and records 8,000 additional draws for each certified deployment from each of the verification and audit pools. The audit split does not represent a chronologically future period.
  \item \textbf{Ingolstadt-21 signal timing.} Six source configurations use 11- and 15-intersection networks. The stylized target simulator has sixteen 21-intersection surge configurations with $d_t=44$. The microscopic comparison uses one fixed \texttt{Ingolstadt21} network and route from RESCO \citep{ault2021reinforcement}, with eight threshold and objective-weight configurations and two seeds per configuration. The calibrated Linux SUMO panel uses $\tau_{\mathrm{rel}}\in[1.032,1.054]$ and 80 audit draws. The manuscript also preserves forty earlier uncalibrated stress runs.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Source Archives, Budget, and Comparators}

The standard archive runs \texttt{Penalty-SPSA}, \texttt{Constrained CMA-ES}, \texttt{Constrained NSGA-II}, and \texttt{Heuristic-Powell} on six source tasks. Their realized cost is 1,428 simulation calls per family. The heterogeneous simulated archive reuses the target panel while changing the source task budgets (26--96 calls), solver subsets, replication schedules (roughly half the records use one replication), and source risk tolerances ($0.02$--$0.10$). It costs 952 source calls per family.

At a given $N$, adaptive arms share an eight-design initialization count, two replications at each initial design and each online step, and up to $(\SlotZeroK,\SlotOneK,\SlotTwoK)$ terminal replications. They share the terminal verifier while providing their own candidate lists. Verification costs can differ through early exit or evaluation of an incumbent. We report actual search-plus-verification calls. Retrospective evaluation and source generation are accounted for separately. STOP uses zero online steps. The main panel has $N\in\{8,15,30,60\}$, the raw-tail and heterogeneous-archive runs use $N\in\{15,60\}$, and calibrated SUMO uses $N=15$.

The six raw-policy comparators are in-house implementations or adaptations: \texttt{SCBO} (isotropic Mat\'ern-5/2 models and trust-region sampling, inspired by \citealt{eriksson2021scalable}), \texttt{MAP-SAAS-CBO} (MAP half-Cauchy sparsity, inspired by \citealt{eriksson2021high}), the custom heteroscedastic GP lookahead arm \texttt{HetGP-KG}, channel-warped \texttt{RGPE} \citep{feurer2018scalable}, the empirical-Bayes multi-task adaptation \texttt{HyperBO-EB} \citep{wang2024pretrained}, and \texttt{Target-SPSA}. \texttt{SCBO-on-Psi} uses the public exposure coordinates with MAP-SAAS and its own trust-region/Sobol/DCT proposals. \texttt{RGPE-on-Psi} uses source and target GPs in these coordinates, its own source templates, and binomial-gate-power scoring. Both have separate cold initialization. \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS} is the target-only public-coordinate control. These arms share access to the public representation and differ in other algorithmic components, including proposals and initialization. \texttt{WarmStart-PatternSearch}, \texttt{Untempered-PriorResid}, and \texttt{Frozen-AFTER} are further system comparisons. The latter restricts which design records inform GP refits while allowing updates to merged repeats and the HVD model.

Cost comparisons first average the paired streams within each task. We report task-level descriptive standard errors and exploratory one-sided Wilcoxon comparisons against frozen GP-FTA-KG, applying Holm adjustment to the six raw-policy comparisons within each family. Certification is a run-level outcome. The independent audit classifies certified deployments by two-sided 95\% Clopper--Pearson intervals. We separately count threshold exceedances of recorded grid verification-law point estimates. An empty same-law field denotes absence of that recheck. Target-run counts across arms, seeds, and budgets describe the ledger size.

\subsection{Independent Evaluation of the Corrected Lookahead}\label{sec:independent_design}

A prospectively fixed panel draws 24 new task configurations each for dual-sourcing inventory, nonstationary queueing, and stylized signal control. The inventory and queueing panels split evenly between the two target horizons. The traffic panel varies the task parameters on the same stylized network. The task-generation seeds and the evaluation streams are disjoint from the earlier experiments. Each new task supplies two paired streams for corrected GP-FTA-KG, GP-FTA-TS, and \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}. The GP-FTA arms reuse their family-specific U3 source archives and fitted default priors. The PublicPsi arm uses the public representation and target data.

Each of the 432 cells has 136 search calls at $N=60$ and up to 380 terminal calls. We meter these calls separately from the independent audits. The budget is 516 calls per cell and actual use depends on the terminal branch. After all candidate lists and terminal outputs are frozen, the audit uses 2,000 independent draws per distinct audited policy under the target law. It reuses a single status-quo audit per task. The estimated exceedance rate and exact confidence intervals describe the certified returns. The analysis averages the paired loss differences across the two streams within each task, reports individual 95\% paired $t$ intervals, and applies Holm adjustment to the six two-sided tests. European storage and microscopic SUMO results remain part of the earlier panel.


\subsection{Matched Prior and Acquisition Comparisons}\label{sec:matched_design}

The matched study draws 24 fresh queueing tasks and 24 fresh traffic DES tasks. The four executed arms use the same two evaluation streams per task, eight source-free, cold-DCT initial designs and the same source-free local proposal rule. After 60 online batches of two observations, each run applies the same terminal verifier, with the guard used when an incumbent occupies slot 1. The 136 search calls and the 380-call verification cap coincide across arms. Posterior models use the same fitted basis, target-based heteroscedastic variance fit, and fixed inactive-mode treatment. For each response, the residual and coefficient-shift variances are $0.10s^2$ and $0.08s^2$, where $s^2$ is the maximum of the variance across initial batch means, their median within-batch mean variance estimate, and $10^{-8}$. The common residual length scale is $0.85$ in standardized coordinates. This design compares the coefficient priors conditional on the shared representation and modeling choices.

\texttt{TargetWeak-GateKG} uses an isotropic coefficient covariance $0.60s^2I$ and a target-initialization intercept. \texttt{HistoryDiag-GateKG} uses the historical coefficient mean and diagonal covariance, and \texttt{HistoryFull-GateKG} retains the same marginal variances together with off-diagonal terms. \texttt{HistoryFull-FeasibleKG} keeps the same model, candidate-design rule, evaluation-set screening and terminal verification, and weights acquisition by posterior chance feasibility. The other arms use the prospective binomial pass probability. The source archive, fitted basis, and coefficient estimates are reused without new fitting.

A fifth policy makes its release decision using the common initialization only. It fits the two constraint models to the first four initial batches, and compares their joint predictive log densities for the other four batch means. It adopts the weak prior when its log score exceeds that of the full prior by more than $\log 10$. This threshold was fixed before the cohort ran. The policy follows the selected arm thereafter. Its values use exact path replay from the two coupled, paid arms on the new tasks. The paired receipts identify the same initialization evidence in those arms.

An independent audit covers each task's status quo once and the nominated and operating decisions as needed, using 2,000 replications per audited policy. Each task's primary endpoint averages its two paired operating losses. We report ordinary 95\% paired $t$ intervals and adjust the two-sided tests jointly across the two families and five comparisons. We also track query revisits, the fixed-batch and pooled constraint variance at audited policies, and whether full covariance changes the operating policy or the audited safety class.

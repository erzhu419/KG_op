\section{Experimental Design and Benchmark Environments}\label{sec:experiments}

To evaluate \textbf{TLS-OLH-KG} without benchmark--representation isomorphism or post-hoc tuning, we enforce a strict two-phase pre-registered protocol across four dynamic stochastic simulation families. All algorithm design choices and hyperparameters ($r = 8$, $\bar{w}=6$, $\rho_{\mathrm{lp}}=0.75$, $\lambda_\perp = 0.04$, $K_{\mathrm{dct}}=12$, verification split $(112, 128, 140)$ at $\delta = 0.05$) were fixed on disjoint development instances prior to opening the confirmation test suite.

\subsection{Four Multi-Period Operational Simulation Families}

In every family, chance feasibility is normalized to $\Prob(Y_{t,g}(x) \le 0) \ge 1 - \alpha$ with $\alpha = 0.05$, and automated unit tests verify that mutating private task laws leaves the public nominal rollout $\mathcal{E}_k(x)$ bit-for-bit invariant ($\|\Delta\psi\|_\infty = 0$):

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Multi-Period Dual-Sourcing Inventory Network DES (\texttt{DynamicInventoryDES}).}
  Over horizon $H_k$ ($d_k = 2 H_k$), policy $x \in [0,1]^{2H_k}$ specifies period-by-period regular base-stock targets (one-period lead time with random $\operatorname{Beta}$-distributed supplier yield) and emergency expediting thresholds (zero-period lead time, premium unit cost). Stochastic demand combines correlated macroeconomic regime shocks across off-peak, shoulder, and promotional peak periods with idiosyncratic period noise. Source tasks operate on shorter horizons $H_s \in \{36, 48\}$ ($d_s \in \{72, 96\}$), whereas held-out target tasks operate on extended horizons $H_t \in \{96, 120\}$ ($d_t \in \{192, 240\}$) under shifted shock covariances and shortage SLAs.

  \item \textbf{Nonstationary Queueing, Staffing, and Surge-Diversion DES (\texttt{DynamicQueueingDES}).}
  Over $H_k$ nonstationary rush-hour slots ($d_k = 2 H_k$), policy $x$ sets primary server staffing capacities $c_h$ and backlog surge-diversion thresholds $\theta^{\mathrm{div}}_h$. Arrivals follow overdispersed Gamma--Poisson bursts modulated by latent diurnal surge factors, with congestion-dependent service efficiency degradation and customer abandonment \citep{halfin1981heavy}. The chance constraint bounds the fraction of slots breaching a critical waiting-queue threshold. Source tasks use $d_s \in \{72, 96\}$; target tasks transfer across horizons to $d_t \in \{192, 240\}$.

  \item \textbf{Real-Data European Power Grid Battery Reserve \& Dispatch (\texttt{OPSDGridStorage}).}
  Built on the DOI-pinned ENTSO-E / Open Power System Data archive \citep{opsd2020timeseries} across 18 European electricity bidding zones, hourly policies $x \in [0,1]^{d_k}$ govern state-of-charge (SOC) reserve targets subject to round-trip efficiency losses ($92\%$), power ratings, day-ahead charging prices, and an unserved-energy reliability chance constraint driven by real non-Gaussian day-ahead load forecast errors. To prevent inter-split autocorrelation leakage while preserving identical seasonal market laws, the 2017--2019 timeline is partitioned into stratified macro-blocks separated by strict $d_k$-hour quarantine buffers across search, independent terminal verification, and post-hoc population audit ($\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{search}} \cap \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{verif}} = \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{verif}} \cap \mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{audit}} = \emptyset$). Six source zones (\texttt{DK\_1, NO\_1, NO\_2, SE\_1, IT\_CNOR, IT\_CSUD} at $d_s \in \{168, 240\}$) train the legacy archive; eight held-out European markets (\texttt{DK\_2, GB\_GBN, IT\_NORD, IT\_SUD, NO\_3, NO\_4, SE\_3, SE\_4} at $d_t = 336$, two-week hourly dispatch) form the confirmation suite.

  \item \textbf{Urban Corridor Signal Timing (\texttt{IngolstadtTrafficDES} \& Live \texttt{libsumo} \texttt{Ingolstadt21}).}
  Parameterized directly by the published RESCO \texttt{ingolstadt21} network \citep{ault2021reinforcement} (up to 21 signalized intersections and $d_t = 44$ main green phases over 1-hour PM peak commuter demand), policies trade off travel delay and cross-junction queue inequity against an HBEFA3 stop-idle-cruise $\text{CO}_2$ emission ceiling. Source tasks optimize smaller 11- and 15-intersection arterial subnetworks ($d_s \in \{23, 31\}$) and transfer to the full 21-intersection network ($d_t = 44$), supplemented by direct microscopic \texttt{libsumo} validation over the 4,283-trip Ingolstadt simulation.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Legacy-Solver Source Archive Protocol, Baselines, and Dual Accounting}

On each family's $M_{\mathrm{src}} = 6$ source environments, four authentic legacy optimizers—\texttt{Penalty-SPSA} \citep{spall1992multivariate}, \texttt{Constrained CMA-ES} \citep{hansen2006cma}, \texttt{Constrained NSGA-II} \citep{deb2002fast}, and \texttt{Heuristic-Powell} pattern search—each execute up to $60$ simulation calls ($238$ realized calls per source task; $B_{\mathrm{src}} = 1{,}428$ total source calls per family), logging their complete adaptive trajectories.

On every target task and replication seed, all competing methods receive the \emph{exact same} online search call budget $B_{\mathrm{search}} = (n_0 + N) m_{\mathrm{batch}}$ ($n_0 = 8$, $m_{\mathrm{batch}} = 2$, $N \in \{15, 30, 60\}$ sequential steps, i.e., $B_{\mathrm{search}} \in \{46, 76, 136\}$ calls) and pass their frozen shortlist through the \emph{exact same} independent terminal verifier:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Cold-Start SOTA:} \texttt{SCBO} \citep{eriksson2021scalable}, \texttt{SAAS-CBO} \citep{eriksson2021high}, target-only heteroscedastic Kriging \texttt{HetGP-KG} \citep{binois2018practical,scott2011correlated}, and direct target \texttt{Penalty-SPSA} / \texttt{Constrained CMA-ES}.
  \item \textbf{External Transfer SOTA (fed the identical $B_{\mathrm{src}}$ legacy archive):} \texttt{RGPE} \citep{feurer2018scalable} and \texttt{HyperBO} \citep{wang2024pretrained} (using linear temporal horizon warping when $d_s \neq d_t$).
  \item \textbf{Mechanistic \& Acquisition Ablations:} \texttt{STOP} ($N=0$ initialization + verifier), \texttt{Untempered-FULL} (testing Theorem~\ref{thm:tempering}(1) variance blow-up), \texttt{Frozen-AFTER} (testing Theorem~\ref{thm:tempering}(1) multi-step stagnation), \texttt{Undebiased-Prior} (testing Theorem~\ref{thm:subspace}(1) selection-bias correction), \texttt{Target-Only-$\psi$-KG} (no source archive), and \texttt{Constrained-TS} / \texttt{cEI} acquisition replacements.
\end{itemize}

Deployed policies are scored post-hoc on independent audit populations ($3{,}000$--$4{,}000$ replications or full-year OPSD windows). Following standard safe-deployment accounting, a certified run incurs its true audit objective $J_t(\hat{x}_t)$, whereas an uncertified run abstains onto a conservative task-level safe fallback cost; we report deployed cost, certification rate, empirical false-certificate count (target: $0$), paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Holm--Bonferroni multiplicity correction, and all-in source-amortized total calls $B_{\mathrm{target}} + B_{\mathrm{src}}/M$.

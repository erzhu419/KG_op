\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}

We use two ledgers for the current method. The first covers 72 newly drawn discrete-event simulation (DES) tasks. The follow-up uses the same DES tasks for paired comparisons and runs the current methods on an established European grid benchmark. State-level prediction and query diagnostics complement these closed-loop experiments.

\subsection{Four Dynamic Operational Simulation Families}

All target tasks enforce business risk threshold $\alpha = 0.05$ and familywise verification level $\delta = 0.05$. Public nominal rollouts produce $\psi(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$, $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x) \in \R_+^4$, and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x) \in \R_+^3$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dual-sourcing inventory control (\texttt{Inventory\_CrossHorizon}).} Regular and expedited replenishment channels ($C_k=2$) balance holding, emergency ordering, and backorder costs under autocorrelated demand shocks and stochastic supply yield. Six source tasks span $d_s\in\{72,96\}$, and targets span $d_t\in\{192,240\}$. Target log-shock drift is scaled by $1.30$ and the regime shock standard deviations by $\sqrt{1.30}$. All $24$ status-quo anchors are classified as safe.
  \item \textbf{Nonstationary staffing and surge diversion (\texttt{Queueing\_CrossHorizon}).} Time-varying staffing and diversion channels ($C_k=2$) face overdispersed arrivals, load-dependent service degradation, and abandonment \citep{halfin1981heavy}. Source dimensions are $72$ and $96$, and target dimensions are $192$ and $240$. Target burst means are scaled by $1.30$ and burst standard deviations by $\sqrt{1.30}$. All $24$ anchors are classified as safe for the critical queue-overflow constraint.
  \item \textbf{European power-grid battery storage (\texttt{OPSD\_EuroGrid}).} We use 2017--2019 load, forecast, and day-ahead price series from Open Power System Data \citep{opsd2020timeseries}. The six source markets have $d_s\in\{168,240\}$. The 14 target markets have $d_t=336$ hourly dispatch variables and were also evaluated in the predecessor study. Within each market, consecutive eligible 48-hour blocks are separated by at least 24 hours and assigned in rotation to the search, verification, and audit pools. All three pools span the same historical period. A target replication concatenates seven blocks sampled with replacement from its designated pool. Independent 8,000-draw rechecks on the verification and audit pools measure sensitivity to the historical blocks used. The follow-up uses the original clipped-tail setting and fresh search and audit streams.
  \item \textbf{Urban arterial signal timing (\texttt{Traffic\_Ingolstadt}).} Built on the RESCO \texttt{Ingolstadt21} signalized network \citep{ault2021reinforcement}, six source subnetworks of 11 and 15 intersections ($d_s \in \{22, 30\}$) transfer to 21-intersection target networks optimizing $d_t = 44$ green phases subject to an HBEFA3 $\mathrm{CO}_2$ chance constraint. In the network discrete-event simulator (DES), target tasks hold nominal arrival forecasts fixed while scaling stochastic platoon-wave log-drift by $1.30\times$ and wave standard deviation by $\sqrt{1.30}$ (\texttt{shift\_scale}$=1.3$). Crucially, a paired anchor audit verifies that the legacy delay-oriented status-quo split violates the $5\%$ $\mathrm{CO}_2$ threshold on all $24/24$ target tasks even at unshifted \texttt{shift\_scale}$=1.0$ (mean violation $61.24\%$, rising to $63.49\%$ at $1.3$), so this benchmark tests compliance restoration of an emissions-uncalibrated legacy timing plan under amplified stochastic volatility alongside cross-subnetwork mismatch. We also run live microscopic \texttt{libsumo} simulations of \texttt{Ingolstadt21} across eight calibrated threshold/weight configurations ($2$ streams each, $N=15$) and forty tight-threshold stress runs.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prospective Four-Domain Controlled Ablations, Meta-Prior Ladder, and Reference Validation}\label{sec:independent_design}

\paragraph{1. Five-arm prospective controlled ablation ($860$ runs across $86$ tasks in all four domains).}
The active runtime (\texttt{fe8df9098424...}) reuses each family's six-task source archive ($1{,}428$ historical calls, zero new source calls). The five-arm comparison comprises $720$ runs on the $72$ DES tasks ($24$ per family) and $140$ runs on the $14$ grid markets. For each task we use two paired streams, $N_0=8$ initial designs, $N=60$ sequential steps and two replications per evaluation, yielding $136$ search calls and a verification cap of $\MaxVerifCalls$ ($516$ calls in total). The five configurations are
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG}: plug-in Fisher-subspace \texttt{SURE} + $\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}}$ + balanced warm-start + \textbf{EC-KG} + guarded three-slot cascade;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-SingleSlotKG}: identical posterior and warm-start, replacing \textbf{EC-KG} with tower-aligned single-slot binomial gate-power KG;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-FeasibleKG}: identical posterior and warm-start, replacing finite-sample binomial cascade VOI with marginal chance-feasibility KG $\Prob_n(Y_{t,g}(x') \le 0)$;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-LOO-ECKG}: identical \textbf{EC-KG} and warm-start, replacing Fisher-subspace \texttt{SURE} and online $\omega_{\mathrm{src}}$ shrinkage with discrete \texttt{LOO-PLL} selection on $\Sigma_{0,c}$;
  \item \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}: cold-start target-only GP on public $\psi(x)$ with gate-power Thompson sampling and local DCT proposals.
\end{itemize}
Inference uses two-sided paired-$t$ tests on stream-averaged task means ($n=24$ per DES family and $n=14$ for grid). For the pooled DES comparison ($n=72$), we scale each family's differences by its across-task standard deviation of status-quo anchor loss. Within each family or pooled comparison, Holm correction covers the four comparators. Wilcoxon tests and win/loss/tie counts are secondary.

\paragraph{2. Cold-start configurations and held-out prediction.}
The follow-up ledger contains $656$ runs: $432$ on the DES tasks (three new cold configurations) and $224$ on grid (the same three plus the five original configurations). Thus $720+656=1{,}376$ runs support both analyses. The plan for the follow-up specifies the primary loss, pairing and Holm families. Its recorded time is 11:38 UTC on November 20, before creation of the follow-up search jobs at 11:40:58 UTC. The earlier $720$-run ledger was sealed at 07:47 UTC that day.

The three cold configurations share the eight initial designs, local DCT proposal rule, target-only within-policy HVD, EC-KG acquisition, and the three-slot verifier. \texttt{ColdPsi} fits a target GP on public $\psi$. \texttt{BasisWeak} uses the source basis and an isotropic weak prior. \texttt{ColdMeta} uses that basis with learned source mean and covariance, plug-in \texttt{SURE}, and empirical residual structure. We report both prespecified comparisons of \texttt{ColdMeta} (with \texttt{ColdPsi} and \texttt{BasisWeak}), adjusting the two $p$-values within each domain. The exploratory comparison with the full warm configuration also changes the initial designs, the use of online source templates, and the source HVD prior.

A separate diagnostic fits each model to the same eight target observations and scores its predictions on the same unobserved policies. The diagonal variant preserves the source mean and coefficient variances. The scrambled variant permutes and sign-flips the source means and uses diagonal coefficient covariance. We also measure the changes in the public summary and in simulated outcomes under policy perturbations.

\paragraph{3. Query diagnostics on frozen working models.}
A separate set of diagnostic rollouts profiles refinement and admission in 96 states, using steps $0$ and $59$ on all queueing and traffic tasks and a screen of up to $24$ of the $160$ candidates. A second study reconstructs 24 states from the first production stream on tasks $t_{00}$--$t_{05}$ in each family at those same steps. The high-precision reference evaluates all $160$ candidates with $256$ outer and $1{,}024$ inner Sobol nodes under the frozen working model. The reference scramble differs from the production grid and is shared across domains for a given task index and stage. It invokes the terminal nominator once on the expanded eligible set in each fantasy, and compares that roster with the original nomination. Production refits the model after an actual observation and recomputes the variance buffer on the terminal set.

\subsection{Companion Matched-Prior and Predecessor Cross-Domain Suites}\label{sec:matched_design}

Two sealed earlier suites complement the prospective experiments: (i)~a $384$-run matched coefficient-prior and counterfactual Slot-0 vs.\ three-slot terminal-replay study on $48$ queueing and traffic tasks under unshrunk $M_{\mathrm{src}}=6$ covariances, paired with leave-one-source-out ($M_{\mathrm{src}}-1=5$) stability and within-policy variance MSE audits; and (ii)~a $9{,}796$-run cross-domain benchmark evaluating predecessor single-slot LOO-PLL \textbf{GP-FTA-KG} and \textbf{GP-FTA-TS} (runtime \texttt{5965482dba30}) at $N \in \{8, 15, 30, 60\}$ against six raw-policy baselines (\texttt{SCBO}, \texttt{MAP-SAAS-CBO}, \texttt{HetGP-KG}, channel-warped \texttt{RGPE}, \texttt{HyperBO-EB}, \texttt{Target-SPSA}), three same-$\psi$ controls (\texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}, \texttt{SCBO-on-Psi}, \texttt{RGPE-on-Psi}), mechanism ablations, a $2{,}108$-run heterogeneous enterprise archive ($\sim 50\%$ $r_a=1$ logs, $\alpha_m \in [0.02, 0.10]$), and $144$ calibrated live \texttt{Ingolstadt21} SUMO runs. A further $40$ earlier uncalibrated stress runs are reported separately (where raw-space \texttt{SCBO} uses $160$-candidate Sobol trust-region sampling with an isotropic Mat\'ern-$5/2$ GP to enforce exact search-call and batch-replication parity with all comparators).

\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}

Our empirical evaluation separates pre-registered prospective experiments on freshly generated target panels from earlier shared-panel benchmarks so that confirmatory claims, mechanism ablations, and cross-domain stress tests are accounted for without selection leakage.

\subsection{Four Dynamic Operational Simulation Families}

All target tasks enforce business risk threshold $\alpha = 0.05$ and familywise verification level $\delta = 0.05$. Public nominal rollouts produce $\psi(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$, $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x) \in \R_+^4$, and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x) \in \R_+^3$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dual-sourcing inventory control (\texttt{Inventory\_CrossHorizon}).} Regular and expedited replenishment channels ($C_k = 2$) balance holding, emergency ordering, and backorder costs under autocorrelated demand shocks and stochastic regular supply yield. Six source tasks operate over $H_s \in \{36, 48\}$ periods ($d_s \in \{72, 96\}$); unseen target tasks scale to $H_t \in \{96, 120\}$ periods ($d_t \in \{192, 240\}$) under a $1.30\times$ demand-volatility regime shift.
  \item \textbf{Nonstationary staffing and surge diversion (\texttt{Queueing\_CrossHorizon}).} Time-varying staffing and admission/diversion channels ($C_k = 2$) face overdispersed nonstationary arrivals, load-dependent service degradation, and customer abandonment \citep{halfin1981heavy}, transferring from $d_s \in \{72, 96\}$ to $d_t \in \{192, 240\}$ under a $1.30\times$ arrival surge while constraining critical queue-overflow probability.
  \item \textbf{European power-grid battery storage (\texttt{OPSD\_EuroGrid}).} Built on real 2017--2019 load, forecast, and day-ahead price series from Open Power System Data \citep{opsd2020timeseries}, six source electricity markets ($d_s \in \{168, 240\}$) transfer to fourteen distinct European target markets ($d_t = 336$ hourly dispatch decisions). Chronologically ordered 48-hour blocks separated by 24-hour isolation buffers rotate deterministically across search, verification, and held-out audit splits; we evaluate both outlier-clamped and unclamped raw-tail error regimes, recording 8,000-draw rechecks on both the verification and audit pools.
  \item \textbf{Urban arterial signal timing (\texttt{Traffic\_Ingolstadt}).} Built on the RESCO \texttt{Ingolstadt21} signalized network \citep{ault2021reinforcement}, six source subnetworks of 11 and 15 intersections ($d_s \in \{22, 30\}$) transfer to 21-intersection target networks optimizing $d_t = 44$ main green phases subject to an HBEFA3 $\mathrm{CO}_2$ chance constraint. Under the $1.30\times$ PM-peak demand surge in the network discrete-event simulator (DES), the pre-surge status-quo timing plan violates the $5\%$ emissions constraint on every target task, testing surge compliance restoration alongside delay reduction. In addition, we evaluate live microscopic \texttt{libsumo} simulations of \texttt{Ingolstadt21} across eight calibrated threshold/weight configurations and forty uncalibrated infeasibility stress runs.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Prospective Factorial Confirmation and 96-State Query Census}\label{sec:independent_design}

\paragraph{1. Pre-registered 720-run prospective factorial panel.}
To test the unified \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} architecture prospectively with zero post-hoc tuning, we draw $24$ fresh unseen target tasks each in \texttt{Inventory\_CrossHorizon}, \texttt{Queueing\_CrossHorizon}, and \texttt{Traffic\_Ingolstadt} ($72$ independent tasks total, disjoint task-generation and simulation seeds from all prior cohorts) while reusing the frozen six-task source archives generated by \texttt{Penalty-SPSA}, \texttt{Constrained CMA-ES}, \texttt{Constrained NSGA-II}, and \texttt{Heuristic-Powell} ($1{,}428$ historical source calls per family; zero new source calls or source refits). Across two paired evaluation streams per task at $N_0 = 8$, $N = 60$, $m_{\mathrm{batch}} = 2$ ($136$ search calls plus up to $\MaxVerifCalls$ verification calls, cap $516$ calls/run), five arms form a clean factorial design ($720$ cells):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG}: Fisher-whitened \texttt{SURE} shrinkage/release + stabilized $\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}}$ + balanced warm-start + \textbf{EC-KG} acquisition + guarded three-slot cascade;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-SingleSlotKG}: identical \texttt{SURE} posterior and warm-start, replacing \textbf{EC-KG} with tower-aligned single-slot binomial gate-power KG (isolating closed-loop multi-slot cascade VOI);
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-FeasibleKG}: identical \texttt{SURE} posterior and warm-start, replacing finite-sample binomial cascade VOI with posterior business chance-feasibility KG $\Prob_n(Y_{t,g}(x') \le 0)$ (isolating finite-sample gate-power alignment);
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-LOO-ECKG}: identical \textbf{EC-KG} acquisition and warm-start, replacing Fisher-whitened \texttt{SURE} + $\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}}$ with the plug-in \texttt{LOO-PLL} selector on $\Sigma_{0,c}^{\mathrm{raw}}$ (isolating Theorem~\ref{thm:sure_uniform} shrinkage/release);
  \item \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}: cold-start target-only GP on public $\psi(x)$ with gate-power Thompson sampling and local DCT proposals (isolating end-to-end legacy transfer value).
\end{itemize}
After Stage~2 seals all deployed policies and abstentions, Stage~3 audits every distinct deployed, primary, and status-quo policy on $2{,}000$ independent replications under $\mathbb{Q}_t$. Paired differences average the two streams within each task ($n = 24$ independent tasks per family, $n = 72$ pooled after standardizing by task-level status-quo dispersion), reporting two-sided paired-$t$ $95\%$ confidence intervals, task win/loss/tie counts, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, and Holm-adjusted two-sided $p$-values across the four comparators.

\paragraph{2. Complete 96-state early and near-terminal query census.}
Across all $24$ queueing tasks and all $24$ traffic tasks on stream~1, we snapshot exact \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} search states at both initialization ($n = 0$, $|\mathcal{D}_0| = 8$) and the penultimate step before clean-up ($n = 59$, $|\mathcal{D}_{59}| \approx 47$--$51$), comparing \textbf{EC-KG}, \texttt{SingleSlotKG}, and \texttt{FeasibleKG} across all $160$ candidate queries in each of the $96$ states.

\subsection{Matched Coefficient-Prior, Source-Deletion, and Four-Family Benchmark Suites}\label{sec:matched_design}

Complementing the prospective panel, three sealed companion studies probe internal mechanisms and cross-domain breadth:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Matched coefficient-prior and terminal-replay study ($384$ runs, $48$ tasks).} On $24$ queueing and $24$ traffic tasks sharing source-free cold-DCT initialization, local proposals, fixed residual/shift scales ($0.10 s^2, 0.08 s^2$), and within-batch HVD noise fits, we compare isotropic weak coefficients (\texttt{TargetWeak-GateKG}), historical diagonal priors (\texttt{HistoryDiag-GateKG}), unshrunk historical full covariances (\texttt{HistoryFull-GateKG} and \texttt{HistoryFull-FeasibleKG}), and an initialization-only predictive release rule (\texttt{InitialRelease-GateKG}), alongside counterfactual Slot-0-only ($K_0=112$) replay on identical search paths and leave-one-source-out ($M_{\mathrm{src}}-1 = 5$) refit stability diagnostics.
  \item \textbf{Shared four-family, heterogeneous-archive, and live-SUMO benchmark ($9{,}796$ runs).} Across \texttt{Inventory\_CrossHorizon}, \texttt{Queueing\_CrossHorizon}, \texttt{OPSD\_EuroGrid} (clamped and unclamped raw tails), and \texttt{Traffic\_Ingolstadt} at $N \in \{8, 15, 30, 60\}$, we benchmark frozen \textbf{GP-FTA-KG} and \textbf{GP-FTA-TS} (runtime \texttt{5965482dba30}) against six raw-policy baselines (\texttt{SCBO}, \texttt{MAP-SAAS-CBO}, \texttt{HetGP-KG}, channel-warped \texttt{RGPE}, \texttt{HyperBO-EB}, \texttt{Target-SPSA}), three same-$\psi$ controls (\texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}, \texttt{SCBO-on-Psi}, \texttt{RGPE-on-Psi}), mechanism ablations (\texttt{WarmStart-PatternSearch}, \texttt{Untempered-PriorResid}, \texttt{Init-Frozen-Transfer}, \texttt{GP-FTA-NoDebias}), a $2{,}108$-run heterogeneous enterprise archive ($r_a \ge 1$ with $\sim 50\%$ single-replication logs, $26$--$96$ calls/task, and $\alpha_m \in [0.02, 0.10]$), and $184$ live microscopic \texttt{Ingolstadt21} SUMO runs (plus $432$ archived single-slot quadrature-aligned runs in Electronic Companion Table~\ref*{tab:independent_panel}).
\end{enumerate}

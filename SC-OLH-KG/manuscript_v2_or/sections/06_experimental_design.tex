\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate the active methods across two closed-loop ledgers—$720$ five-arm runs on $72$ discrete-event simulation (DES) tasks and $656$ follow-up cold-configuration and $336$-dimensional European power-grid runs—complemented by a two-stage cross-policy propagation study ($86$ exploratory tasks plus $72$ pre-registered fresh unseen tasks), a $24$-scramble frozen-state reference and \texttt{cEI} audit, and $9{,}796$ predecessor benchmark runs.

\subsection{Four Dynamic Operational Simulation Families}

All target tasks enforce business risk threshold $\alpha = 0.05$ and familywise verification level $\delta = 0.05$. Public nominal rollouts produce $\psi(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$, $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x) \in \R_+^4$, and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x) \in \R_+^3$. Across all three synthetic DES families, target tasks hold public nominal forecasts unchanged while applying private regime shock multiplier \texttt{shift\_scale}$=1.3$, multiplying three-regime private log-shock or burst drift means by $1.30$ and regime shock standard deviations by $\sqrt{1.30}$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dual-sourcing inventory control (\texttt{Inventory\_CrossHorizon}).} Regular and expedited replenishment channels ($C_k=2$) balance holding, emergency ordering, and backorder costs under autocorrelated demand shocks and stochastic supply yield. Six source tasks span $d_s\in\{72,96\}$, and $24$ target tasks span $d_t\in\{192,240\}$. Independent $2{,}000$-draw audits classify all $24$ status-quo anchors as safe.
  \item \textbf{Nonstationary staffing and surge diversion (\texttt{Queueing\_CrossHorizon}).} Time-varying staffing and diversion channels ($C_k=2$) face overdispersed arrivals, load-dependent service degradation, and abandonment \citep{halfin1981heavy}. Six source tasks have $d_s \in \{72, 96\}$, and $24$ target tasks have $d_t \in \{192, 240\}$. All $24$ anchors are classified as safe for the critical queue-overflow chance constraint.
  \item \textbf{European power-grid battery storage (\texttt{OPSD\_EuroGrid}).} Using 2017--2019 load, forecast, and day-ahead price series from Open Power System Data \citep{opsd2020timeseries}, six source markets ($d_s\in\{168,240\}$) transfer to $14$ target markets ($d_t=336$ hourly dispatch variables, matching the market roster of the predecessor study). Within each market, consecutive eligible 48-hour blocks are separated by at least 24 hours and assigned in rotation to search, verification, and audit pools spanning the same 2017--2019 period. Each target replication concatenates seven blocks sampled with replacement from its designated pool; independent $8{,}000$-draw rechecks on both verification and audit pools quantify sensitivity across interleaved historical blocks under fresh search and audit streams.
  \item \textbf{Urban arterial signal timing (\texttt{Traffic\_Ingolstadt}).} Built on the RESCO \texttt{Ingolstadt21} signalized network \citep{ault2021reinforcement}, six source subnetworks of 11 and 15 intersections ($d_s \in \{22, 30\}$) transfer to 21-intersection target networks optimizing $d_t = 44$ green phases under an HBEFA3 $\mathrm{CO}_2$ chance constraint. Paired anchor audits show that the legacy delay-oriented status-quo split violates the $5\%$ $\mathrm{CO}_2$ threshold on all $24/24$ target tasks even at unshifted \texttt{shift\_scale}$=1.0$ (mean violation $61.24\%$, rising to $63.49\%$ at $1.3$), so this benchmark tests compliance restoration of an emissions-uncalibrated legacy plan under amplified volatility and subnetwork mismatch. Companion live microscopic \texttt{libsumo} simulations of \texttt{Ingolstadt21} evaluate eight calibrated threshold/weight configurations ($2$ streams each, $N=15$) alongside $40$ tight-threshold uncalibrated stress runs.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Closed-Loop Ablations, Direct Cross-Policy Propagation, and Multi-Scramble Reference Validation}\label{sec:independent_design}

\paragraph{1. Five-arm controlled ablation ($860$ runs across $86$ tasks in all four domains).}
The sealed active runtime (\texttt{fe8df9098424...}) reuses each family's six-task source archive ($1{,}428$ historical calls, zero new source calls), running $720$ evaluations on the $72$ DES tasks ($24$ per family) and $140$ evaluations on the $14$ grid markets ($2$ paired streams per task, $N_0=8$ initial designs, $N=60$ steps, $m_{\mathrm{batch}}=2$ replications per step, $136$ search calls, and verification cap $\MaxVerifCalls$ for $516$ maximum calls per run):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG}: plug-in Fisher-subspace \texttt{SURE} + $\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}}$ + balanced warm-start + \textbf{EC-KG} + guarded three-slot cascade;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-SingleSlotKG}: identical posterior and warm-start, replacing \textbf{EC-KG} with tower-aligned single-slot binomial gate-power KG;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-FeasibleKG}: identical posterior and warm-start, replacing finite-sample binomial cascade VOI with tower-aligned one-step KG weighted by Gaussian marginal chance-feasibility probability $\Prob_n\big(g_t(x') \le \Phi^{-1}(\alpha)\sqrt{\hat{v}_{n,g}^{\mathrm{buf}}(x')}\big)$ under total posterior constraint variance $\sigma_{n,g}^2(x') + 0.25 r_{\perp,g}(x')$;
  \item \texttt{GP-FTA-LOO-ECKG}: identical \textbf{EC-KG} and warm-start, replacing Fisher-subspace \texttt{SURE} and online $\omega_{\mathrm{src}}$ shrinkage with discrete \texttt{LOO-PLL} selection on $\Sigma_{0,c}$;
  \item \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}: cold-start target-only GP on public $\psi(x)$ with gate-power Thompson sampling and local DCT proposals (varying source prior, initialization, and acquisition simultaneously as an end-to-end target-only reference).
\end{itemize}
Primary inference uses two-sided paired-$t$ tests on stream-averaged task means ($n=24$ per DES family, $n=14$ for grid, and $n=72$ standardized by family anchor-loss SD for pooled DES) with four-comparator Holm adjustment per block.

\paragraph{2. Cold-start meta-prior ladder and two-panel direct cross-policy propagation.}
The $656$-run follow-up ledger ($432$ cold-start DES runs plus $224$ grid runs; pre-registered at 11:38 UTC on November 20 ahead of job creation at 11:40:58 UTC) compares three cold configurations sharing identical $N_0=8$ cold-DCT initial designs, local DCT proposals, target-only within-policy HVD, \textbf{EC-KG}, and three-slot verification: \texttt{ColdPsi} (target GP on public $\psi$), \texttt{BasisWeak} (source basis $\tilde{\phi}$ with isotropic weak prior), and \texttt{ColdMeta} (source basis, learned $(\mu_{0,c}, \widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}})$, plug-in \texttt{SURE}, and $w_{\perp,c}$), alongside an exploratory multi-component contrast against full warm \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG}.

While static $8$-observation rank correlations measure prior fit, they do not isolate how observing a \emph{new} target policy updates predictions at \emph{other, unobserved} policies. We therefore conduct a direct before-to-after cross-policy propagation experiment in two stages: an exploratory study on the existing $86$ tasks across all four domains (\texttt{exploratory\_86}), followed by a pre-registered one-shot confirmation (\texttt{PREREGISTRATION\_REV5\_PROPAGATION\_CONFIRM\_20261121.json}) on $72$ newly generated unseen DES tasks (\texttt{confirm\_fresh\_72}: $24$ inventory, $24$ queueing, and $24$ traffic tasks generated with disjoint target seeds offset by $+1{,}000{,}000$; all $20$ usable OPSD markets were already exhausted). On every task, all compared models observe the \emph{exact same} $N_0=8$ cold-DCT policies (plus a scarce $2$-policy subset), share identical public $\psi(x)$, identical source-learned active basis $\tilde{\phi}(\psi(x))$, and identical target-only within-policy HVD, and update on the same four probe policies selected deterministically by public active-basis cosine leverage before scoring $32$ disjoint held-out policies ($400$ audit replications each). Family~A isolates pure kernel-mediated propagation by setting active source means to zero ($\mu_{0,\mathrm{act}} \equiv 0$) and matching active-subspace trace across four priors—\texttt{Kernel-FullSource} ($\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,\mathrm{act}}^{\mathrm{stab}}$), \texttt{Kernel-DiagSource} ($\operatorname{diag}(\widehat{\Sigma}_{0,\mathrm{act}}^{\mathrm{stab}})$), \texttt{Kernel-IsoBasis} (isotropic on $\tilde{\phi}$), and \texttt{Kernel-PermutedScale} (deranging the learned diagonal variances across active modes while preserving the exact multiset of marginal scales)—while Family~B compares online adaptive \texttt{Online-FullSURE} and \texttt{Online-DiagSURE} against \texttt{Online-SameBasisWeak} and \texttt{Online-PermutedScaleSURE}.

\paragraph{3. Multi-scramble frozen-state reference and \texttt{UB-cEI} audit.}
Complementing the $96$-state menu-refinement/admission census, we reconstruct the $24$ production states from stream~0 on tasks $t_{00}$--$t_{05}$ at steps $0$ and $59$ in queueing and traffic. Beyond the single-scramble all-$160$ $256 \times 1{,}024$ Sobol reference, we evaluate key contenders across $R = 24$ independent $(256 \times 1{,}024)$ Sobol scrambles per state to obtain Monte Carlo standard errors and $95\%$ confidence intervals on both menu VOI and unconditional single-nomination gain $\Delta_n^{\mathrm{term}}(q)$, audit Stage~A missed-winner anatomy, and compare \textbf{EC-KG} directly against \citet[Section~5]{ungredda2024bayesian}'s sampled-incumbent \texttt{UB-cEI} (marginal feasibility probability times expected improvement over the best sampled candidate with $\Prob_n(Y_{t,g} \le 0) \ge 1-\alpha$, falling back to maximum feasibility probability if none qualifies) and gate-power \texttt{Gate-cEI}.

\subsection{Companion Matched-Prior and Predecessor Cross-Domain Suites}\label{sec:matched_design}

Two sealed earlier suites provide broader context: (i)~a $384$-run matched coefficient-prior and counterfactual Slot-0 vs.\ three-slot terminal-replay study on $48$ queueing and traffic tasks under unshrunk $M_{\mathrm{src}}=6$ covariances, paired with leave-one-source-out ($M_{\mathrm{src}}-1=5$) and variance-MSE audits; and (ii)~a $9{,}796$-run cross-domain benchmark evaluating predecessor single-slot LOO-PLL \textbf{GP-FTA-KG}/\textbf{TS} (runtime \texttt{5965482dba30}) at $N \in \{8, 15, 30, 60\}$ against six raw-policy baselines (\texttt{SCBO}, \texttt{MAP-SAAS-CBO}, \texttt{HetGP-KG}, channel-warped \texttt{RGPE}, \texttt{HyperBO-EB}, \texttt{Target-SPSA}), three same-$\psi$ controls, mechanism ablations, a $2{,}108$-run heterogeneous enterprise archive ($\sim 50\%$ $r_a=1$ logs, $\alpha_m \in [0.02, 0.10]$), $144$ calibrated live \texttt{Ingolstadt21} SUMO runs, and $40$ tight-threshold stress runs (with raw-space \texttt{SCBO} using $160$-candidate Sobol trust-region sampling for exact search-call parity).

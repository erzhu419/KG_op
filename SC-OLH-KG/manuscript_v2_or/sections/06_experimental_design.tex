\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}

We evaluate the framework across paired closed-loop studies on five stochastic operational simulation families, complemented by terminal contract and verification-gate experiments, a pre-registered unseen-task posterior calibration and structural ablation suite, and $24$-scramble frozen-state lookahead diagnostics (provenance hashes and pre-registration records appear in Electronic Companion Section~\ref*{ec:provenance}).

\subsection{Five Stochastic Operational Simulation Families}

All target tasks enforce violation threshold $\alpha = 0.05$ and familywise verification level $\delta = 0.05$. Within the four domain-engineered benchmarks, deterministic public nominal rollouts produce $\psi(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$, $A_{\mathrm{local}}(x) \in \R_+^4$, and $N_{\mathrm{shared}}(x) \in \R_+^3$ (Section~\ref{sec:domain_invariant_psi} and Table~\ref{tab:psi_inventory}; Electronic Companion Section~\ref*{ec:psi_specs}), while the fifth benchmark uses a simulator-free black-box control-profile summary $\psi^{\mathrm{generic}}(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$. Each family fits its source prior on six within-family historical tasks ($M_{\mathrm{src}}=6$ at baseline, expanded to $M_{\mathrm{src}} \in \{3, 4, 6, 12\}$ with exact $\omega_M = p/(M_{\mathrm{src}}+p)$ in scaling experiments). Across synthetic discrete-event simulation (DES) families, baseline target tasks hold public forecasts fixed while multiplying three-regime private drift means by $1.30$ and shock standard deviations by $\sqrt{1.30}$ (tested across multipliers $\{1.0, 1.3, 1.6\}$).
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Dual-sourcing inventory control.} Regular and expedited replenishment channels ($C_k=2$) balance holding, emergency ordering, and backorder costs under autocorrelated demand shocks and stochastic supply yield ($6$ source tasks at $d_s\in\{72,96\}$; $24$ target tasks at $d_t\in\{192,240\}$; all $24$ status-quo anchors safe under $2{,}000$-draw audits).
  \item \textbf{Nonstationary staffing and surge diversion.} Time-varying staffing and diversion channels ($C_k=2$) face overdispersed arrivals, load-dependent service degradation, and abandonment \citep{halfin1981heavy} ($6$ source tasks at $d_s \in \{72, 96\}$; $24$ target tasks at $d_t \in \{192, 240\}$; all $24$ anchors safe).
  \item \textbf{European power-grid battery storage.} Using 2017--2019 load, forecast, and day-ahead price series from Open Power System Data \citep{opsd2020timeseries}, $6$ source markets ($d_s\in\{168,240\}$) transfer to $14$ target markets ($d_t=336$ hourly dispatch variables). Consecutive $48$-hour blocks separated by at least $24$-hour buffers rotate across search, verification, and audit pools within the same period; each target replication concatenates seven blocks drawn with replacement from its designated pool, and $8{,}000$-draw rechecks on both verification and audit pools quantify sensitivity across interleaved historical blocks.
  \item \textbf{Urban arterial signal timing.} Built on the RESCO \texttt{Ingolstadt21} signalized network \citep{ault2021reinforcement}, $6$ source subnetworks of $11$ and $15$ intersections ($d_s \in \{22, 30\}$) transfer to $21$-intersection target networks optimizing $d_t = 44$ green phases under an HBEFA3 $\mathrm{CO}_2$ chance constraint. Because the legacy delay-oriented status-quo plan violates the $5\%$ $\mathrm{CO}_2$ threshold on all $24/24$ target tasks even without shift ($61.24\%$ mean violation, $63.49\%$ at $1.3\times$ shift), this benchmark tests compliance restoration of an emissions-uncalibrated legacy plan (supplemented by live microscopic \texttt{SUMO} runs in Electronic Companion Section~\ref*{ec:archived_sensitivity}).
  \item \textbf{Author-constructed tandem queueing--newsvendor benchmark with generic $\psi^{\mathrm{generic}}$.} Motivated by tandem queueing and continuous newsvendor problems in the simulation-optimization literature \citep[distinct from SimOpt's single-period Burr~XII newsvendor;][]{koenig1985procedure,hong2015chance,eckman2023diagnostic}, we construct a self-contained four-channel synthetic benchmark ($C_k=4$) coupling an upstream raw-material replenishment stage with three serial service stations under finite-buffer blocking across $W_k$ windows ($6$ source tasks at $d_s \in \{24, 32\}$; $24$ target tasks at $d_t \in \{48, 64\}$; complete dynamics in Electronic Companion Section~\ref*{ec:psi_specs}). Crucially, $\psi^{\mathrm{generic}}(x) \in \R^{\PsiDim}$ uses only channel means, quantiles, temporal ramping norms, and public arrival-forecast tertiles—without any tandem blocking or carryover equations—testing usability when no nominal simulator recursion is available.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Experimental Blocks and Statistical Inference Hierarchy}\label{sec:independent_design}

\paragraph{1. Controlled mechanism ablations, search comparators, and audited reference ratio (\texttt{Ref-400}).}
Across all $86$ four-domain target tasks ($72$ DES tasks plus $14$ grid markets, $2$ paired random streams per task, $N_0=8$, $N=60$, $m_{\mathrm{batch}}=2$ for $136$ search calls, and shared three-slot clean-up cap $\MaxVerifCalls$), we compare \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} against two pre-specified four-comparator blocks:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Constrained and meta-learning search comparators (identical search budget and guarded three-slot clean-up):} (i)~raw-space trust-region constrained BO (\texttt{SCBO-3Slot}; \citealp{eriksson2021scalable}); (ii)~latent-integrated noisy expected improvement (\texttt{GP-FTA-LatentNEI-3Slot}, averaging $32$ antithetic joint posterior draws that recompute the feasible sampled incumbent per realization; \citealp{letham2019constrained,ungredda2024bayesian}); (iii)~bootstrap ranking-weighted GP ensemble on shared $\psi(x)$ with cold initialization and online source-template proposals (\texttt{RGPE-on-Psi-3Slot}; \citealp{feurer2018scalable}); and (iv)~a pooled source-mean plus target-discrepancy GP paired with gate-power KG on $\psi(x)$ (\texttt{PooledSourceResGP-KG-3Slot}, a pooled-archive warm-start variant motivated by \citealp{poloczek2017multi} without source-index coregionalization). Five arms (\textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG}, \texttt{FeasibleKG}, \texttt{LatentNEI-3Slot}, \texttt{SCBO-3Slot}, and \texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS}) are also evaluated on the $24$-task generic-$\psi$ tandem queueing--newsvendor benchmark.
  \item \emph{Internal mechanism block:} (i)~\texttt{GP-FTA-FeasibleKG} (replacing binomial cascade VOI with marginal chance-feasibility KG $\Prob_n(g_t(x') \le \Phi^{-1}(\alpha)\sqrt{\hat{v}_{n,g}^{\mathrm{buf}}(x')})$, testing gate-power alignment); (ii)~\texttt{GP-FTA-SingleSlotKG} (tower-aligned single-slot binomial gate-power KG, isolating incremental multi-slot lookahead); (iii)~\texttt{GP-FTA-LOO-ECKG} (replacing Fisher-subspace \texttt{SURE} with discrete \texttt{LOO-PLL}); and (iv)~\texttt{PublicPsi-TargetGP-TS} (end-to-end target-only GP on $\psi$ with gate-power Thompson sampling and local DCT proposals).
\end{itemize}
To scale absolute improvements without claiming a global optimum, a fixed high-budget audited reference (\texttt{Ref-400}: $400$ multi-scale DCT, template, and Sobol candidates screened with $12$ replications plus random DCT local perturbations, selecting the best-found policy with $\hat{p}_t^{\mathrm{audit}}(x) \le 0.05$ on the task audit stream) defines reference gain ratio $(\bar{J}_{\mathrm{SQ}} - \bar{\calL}_{\mathrm{SQ}})/(\bar{J}_{\mathrm{SQ}} - \bar{J}_{\mathrm{Ref\text{-}400}})$. Primary inference uses two-sided paired-$t$ tests on stream-averaged task means ($n=24$ per DES/tandem family, $n=14$ for grid, and $n=72$ standardized by family anchor SD for pooled DES) with four-comparator Holm adjustment within each block, supplemented by across-task \textbf{EC-KG} SD scaling, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, and compliance penalty weights $\lambda_{\mathrm{pen}} \in \{1, 2, 5, 10\}$.

\paragraph{2. Pure single-factor terminal counterfactuals, closed-loop variants, gate design, and frozen-state audits.}
To isolate terminal governance and shortlist nomination without confounding search trajectories, we evaluate two pure single-factor counterfactuals directly on the $172$ frozen terminal \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} states $\mathcal{F}_N$: (i)~\texttt{TermOnly-AllowUnvisited}, keeping the $60$-step \textbf{EC-KG} search trajectory identical and relaxing only $\hat{x}_t \in \mathcal{D}_N$ to $\mathcal{D}_N \cup \mathcal{C}_N^{\mathrm{unvis}}$; and (ii)~\texttt{TermOnly-UnthreshMaxU}, maximizing working-model cascade utility $U_N(\mathcal{S})$~\eqref{eq:working_cascade_utility} over all distinct ordered triples of top-$14$ evaluated candidates across guarded and unguarded modes without $0.55$ gate thresholds, $0.5/0.5$ blending, or fixed Slot-1 rules. Alongside these, three closed-loop variants modify search and terminal rules jointly: \texttt{Unvisited+Slot0KG} (single-slot gate-power KG search plus unvisited terminal eligibility), \texttt{RestrictedMaxU} (online utility-based search/nomination fixing Slot~1 and scoring top Slot-0/Slot-2 subsets), and \texttt{GP-FTA-LongSearch-k0} ($N=100$ search steps paired with curtailed $k^\star = 0$ gates $(72, 82, 90)$). Across all $172$ terminal \textbf{GP-FTA-ECKG} states, we evaluate exact binomial gates at $k^\star \in \{0, 1, 2, 3\}$ ($(72, 82, 90)$ through $(173, 187, 198)$), comparing both replay-computed curtailed call counts and live replication-by-replication streaming verification wall-clock times at default $k^\star=1$. On $24$ reconstructed queueing and traffic states (steps~$0$ and~$59$), $R=24$ independent $(256\times 1{,}024)$ Sobol scrambles re-evaluate the full-bank reference winner alongside \textbf{EC-KG}, \texttt{SingleSlotKG}, \texttt{FeasibleKG}, posterior-mean \texttt{PlugIn-cEI}, $64$-draw \texttt{LatentNEI}, and HVD buffer perturbations $\{0.15, 0.35, 0.55\}$.

\paragraph{3. Cold-start ladder, unbiased gate-power calibration, component ablations, and exact-$\omega_M$ scaling.}
To separate functional priors from warm-starting in closed loop, $432$ cold-start DES runs and $224$ grid runs compare \texttt{ColdPsi} (target-only GP on $\psi$), \texttt{BasisWeak} (learned basis $\tilde{\phi}$ with isotropic weak prior), and \texttt{ColdMeta} (learned $(\tilde{\phi}, \mu_{0,c}, \widehat{\Sigma}_{0,c}^{\mathrm{stab}})$ with \texttt{SURE}) under identical $N_0=8$ cold-DCT designs, local DCT proposals, target-only HVD, and \textbf{EC-KG}. To evaluate prior-informed posterior shape and decision-relevant calibration without confounding warm-starts, an $86$-task exploratory panel and a pre-registered $72$-task unseen DES confirmation panel (shared across Panels~A and~B of Table~\ref{tab:direct_prop}) hold $\psi(x)$, $\tilde{\phi}(\psi(x))$, $N_0=8$ cold-DCT observations, and target-only HVD identical across models, average four single-step $8 \to 9$ leverage-probe updates per task, and score $32$ disjoint held-out policies ($400$ audit draws each, violation count $V$). Because plug-in $C_{112,1}(V/400)$ exhibits finite-sample Jensen bias, we score gate-power calibration primarily via the exact minimum-variance unbiased hypergeometric U-statistic $\widehat{C}_U(V) = [\binom{400-V}{112} + V\binom{400-V}{111}]/\binom{400}{112}$—whose paired squared-error differences $\E[(a - \widehat{C}_U)^2 - (b - \widehat{C}_U)^2] = (a - C_{112,1}(p))^2 - (b - C_{112,1}(p))^2$ have exact zero finite-audit bias (\texttt{unbiased\_target\_preserves\_mse\_diff})—alongside $400$-draw audit-plug-in RMSE, feasibility Brier score and AUROC ($52/72$ tasks with both classes), and unbiased Slot-0 saving. On the $72$ unseen tasks, we evaluate zero-mean trace-matched kernel variants (\texttt{FullSource}, \texttt{DiagSource}, \texttt{IsoBasis}, \texttt{PermutedScale}), online \texttt{FullSURE} against \texttt{ColdPsi-GP}, \texttt{Online-SameBasisWeak}, fixed-dictionary empirical-Bayes BLR (\texttt{EB-LinearDict-on-Psi}), pooled source-mean plus target-discrepancy GP (\texttt{PooledSourceResGP-on-Psi}), genuine $64$-bootstrap ranking-weighted \texttt{RGPE-BootstrapRank-on-Psi} \citep{feurer2018scalable} and \texttt{Fixed50-Ensemble-on-Psi}, four implementation-component ablations (\texttt{Comp-NoLowPass}, \texttt{Comp-NoDebias}, \texttt{Comp-NoOrtho}, \texttt{Comp-NoSparse}), $\psi$ ablations (\texttt{Psi-DropVol7D}, forecast-fed HVD-controlled \texttt{Psi-GenericPhysOnly}, and \texttt{Psi-GenericForecastFull}), $15\%$ forecast noise, shift scales $\{1.0, 1.3, 1.6\}$, and exact-$\omega_M = p/(M_{\mathrm{src}}+p)$ scaling across horizon-stratified $M_{\mathrm{src}}=3$ ($2+1$) and horizon-balanced $M_{\mathrm{src}} \in \{4, 6, 12\}$ ($2+2$, $3+3$, $6+6$).
